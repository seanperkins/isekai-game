# Areas, part three: the sacred cluster (F2c) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fourteen new rooms above and east of the Deep, built by the F2a builder: a cemetery (four rooms), a cathedral (five) and a crypt (five), joined to the Deep at D7's west door and to D5 by a coffin slide.

**Architecture:** Three area modules registered after the village: `CemeteryArea` (prefix `CM`, area `cemetery`), `CathedralArea` (`CA`, `sacred`), `CryptArea` (`CR`, `crypt`). The later modules link to the earlier modules' rooms. Art is borrowed (cemetery and sacred from the cave, crypt from the deep).

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), `tools/build_areas.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/research/areas-f2.md`, `docs/research/area-level-design.md` section 11.

## Global Constraints

- The builder never writes a world that has a `WorldValidator` error or a `RoomLint` finding; a re-run changes nothing (`tools/build_areas.sh --check`).
- Shipped rooms change only by the exits (and marked features) the modules attach: D7 gains a west door (ungated: the cluster is as far behind the wall cling as D7 is), D5 gains a top exit. D6, the Taratect's arena, gains nothing: a boss room has exactly one exit.
- Spawns are creatures that exist now (support monsters that name the area in the design table); twins are placed in F2f. Holy, taint and safe zones, traps and the priests' emitter are not built: art and text until a later piece. The Churchyard is the truce: a glow pool, a tablet, no altar and no spawns.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- D7 stays a valid boss antechamber (one pool, no spawns, its exit to D6 unchanged) and D6 is untouched.
- The coffin slide is a drop: D5 cannot be climbed back out of, and nothing stands over the hole in the Ossuary's floor.
- Without wall cling the cemetery cluster is not reachable (`reachable(skip_gated)` leaves it out) and every cluster room is reachable with it.
- The Churchyard and no other cemetery room has no spawns.

## Layout (cells; one cell is 640 by 360)

```
row 0:            Loft 2x1 (20..21) -- Bell Shaft 1x3 (22, rows 0..2)
row 3:  Nave 3x1 (19..21)  -- Transept (22) -- Font Chapel (23)       the Transept's top is the Bell Shaft's floor
row 4:  Gate Stair 1x2 (17, rows 4..5) -- Gate Tunnel (18) -- Churchyard 2x1 (19..20) -- Lych Gate (21) -- Crypt Stair 1x2 (22, rows 4..5)
row 5:  Gate Stair's lower door onto D7 (18);  Ossuary 2x1 (23..24) -- Niche Gallery 3x1 (25..27) -- Vault (28)
row 6:  Coffin Slide 2x1 (23..24) under the Ossuary, over D5 (23, row 7)
```
