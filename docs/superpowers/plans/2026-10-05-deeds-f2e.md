# The nine deeds that wait on rooms (F2e) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every deed in `Deeds.RESERVED` (`defeated.moonlit`, `barrier_broken`, `hidden_found`, `gap_crossed`, `lamp_drunk`, `vigil_kept`, `room_held`, `village_visited_unseen`, `village_kept`) is emitted by real play in a room that exists, and `Deeds.RESERVED` is empty.

**Architecture:** `RoomDef.tags` (`moonlit`, `hold`) and five new feature kinds (`barrier`, `hidden`, `gap`, `lamp`, `vigil`), each built by `RoomFeatures.make` into a small node that reports its deed on the event bus, once per life where the doc says so (the rules ledger answers "already this life": `SkillRules.count(event, {"id": id})`). Village deeds are tracked by a `VillageWatch` node that `Game` keeps. The area modules place them with `AreaKit` helpers; the builder writes the rooms.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), `tools/build_areas.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/research/areas-f2.md` (the nine deeds), `scripts/core/deeds.gd`, `tools/form_gates.json` (what waits).

## Global Constraints

- Shipped (hand-authored) rooms do not change; the new features and tags go only in generated rooms through the area modules.
- A deed fires once per life per feature id (gap, lamp, vigil), once per life per room (hold) and once per life (the two village deeds): a repeat never counts twice for a gate that wants n.
- No new deed events or tag keys beyond `id` on `gap_crossed`, `lamp_drunk` and `vigil_kept`. No altars. No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.
- Nothing a player must pass is placed behind a barrier or a hidden door: they stand in front of side rooms and a rare vault only.

## Review Focus

- A feature a deed waits on is placed somewhere a player can reach it with the base kit (side rooms included), never in a room the world cannot reach.
- A barrier or a hidden door never closes the only way into a room the world needs (the Hunting Blind, the Cooled Vault, the Font Chapel and the Reliquary are dead ends).
- Nothing double-counts: a second pass through a gap, a second drink at a lamp, a second hold of a room, a second leaving of the village in one life emits nothing more.
- The village deeds fire only on leaving the village; killing a village creature or alerting one after the first visit spoils `unseen`/`kept` for the life.
- A room with a new feature or tag still loads in the room editor, saves and reads back equal.
