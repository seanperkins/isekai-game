# Areas, part four: the volcano and the demon area (F2d) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Fourteen new rooms built by the F2a builder: a volcano (seven rooms) below the Deep's D4 and a demon area (seven rooms) east of the crypt's Vault.

**Architecture:** Two area modules registered after the crypt: `VolcanoArea` (prefix `VO`, area `volcano`) and `DemonArea` (prefix `DM`, area `demon`). Art is borrowed (volcano from the cave, demon from the deep). `AreaWorlds.ORDER` gains both prefixes.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), `tools/build_areas.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/research/areas-f2.md`, `docs/research/area-level-design.md` sections 7 and 13.

## Global Constraints

- The builder never writes a world that has a `WorldValidator` error or a `RoomLint` finding; a re-run changes nothing (`tools/build_areas.sh --check`).
- Shipped rooms change only by the exits (and marked features) the modules attach: D4 gains a floor hole into the volcano; the Vault (CR4) gains an east door (a generated room, but built by an earlier module).
- A label on a drop does not hold a walker (a hole in a floor lets anyone fall through): a second way into a gated area is a `shortcut` shut until a far-side switch opens it; a way up that no ledge reaches is not built.
- Spawns are creatures that exist now (support monsters that name the area in the design table); twins are placed in F2f. Heat, taint, ward and curse rules, spawn gates, the pact altar and the traps are not built: art and text until a later piece. No altars: pools only. `heat_resist` is not a gate label yet, so the Cooled Vault is a plain side room.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- Every way up in a new room (a climb to a ceiling opening, a stair to a door) is walkable on the base jump: each ledge within a hop of the last, the last within a hop of the door or ending in the opening.
- The Ash Cut's ceiling hole is shut in play until its switch is pulled, and the Ash Cut stays reachable from the Forge Row without it.
- Without the Deep's gates (`skip_gated`) neither area is reachable and the rest of the world is as it was.
- No spawn stands over a floor hole; the Throne's creatures are the caller and what it emits.
