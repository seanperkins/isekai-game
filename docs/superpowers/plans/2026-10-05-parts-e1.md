# Parts, grafts and remains (E1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A kill can leave a part; the player picks it up and carries it; the undead graft the ones that fit onto their body for a passive; a hard hit knocks a graft off; a death leaves everything carried as remains in the room to recover in the next life. The deeds `part_harvested`, `graft_attached`, `graft_shed`, `item_carried` and `corpse_left` are emitted, so the gates of E1 in `Deeds.RESERVED` can be met.

**Architecture:** `PartDef` data (`data/parts/*.tres`, from `tools/parts.json` by `tools/build_parts.py`). `CreatureDef.drops` and a pure `Drops.roll`. A per-life `Inventory` (parts carried, grafts worn, slot rules, the passives as stat modifiers). `PartPickup` nodes where an enemy goes down. `Player` owns the inventory, collects, grafts (undead only), sheds on a heavy hit and drops everything at death into `WorldProgress.remains`; `Game` puts a `Remains` pickup back in that room.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), Python generators.

**Spec:** `docs/research/species-form-branches.md` (the parts table at the end, section 4 on grafts) and `docs/isekai-chronicles-design-doc.md` (a graft is a passive plus a nudge toward a path).

## Global Constraints

- The ten parts: ribs, flesh, claw, weapon_arm, bow_arm, skull, focus, wrappings (grafts), phylactery_shard (held), moonstone (key). Worn limits per part: claw 2, wrappings 2, the rest 1; at most `Inventory.GRAFT_CAP` = 4 grafts worn. A part that cannot be worn stays carried; carried parts are unlimited.
- Only the undead wear grafts. Any species carries parts; a part's deed is the same either way.
- Drop chances (design table): ribs and flesh from zombie and ghoul twins and from any large corpse (`max_hp >= DeedTags.HEAVY_HP`, 0.2); claw from gloom_wolf, bog_lizardman, ghoul twins; weapon_arm from shield_guard, Bone Warrior and Draugr twins; bow_arm from skeletal archer twins and village_goblin; skull from skeleton; focus from goblin_shaman and chapel_priest; wrappings from Mummy-Priest twins; phylactery_shard from Lich twins (0.1); moonstone from moon_hare (0.15). Other rolls 0.3.
- A graft sheds when one hit takes at least a quarter of the player's max HP; it falls beside the player as a pickup.
- Death: everything carried and worn becomes remains in the room, once; a later death replaces them. Remains also vanish after three deaths unrecovered.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits.

## Review Focus

- A part picked up twice in a frame, a pickup collected by something that is not the player, and a full body (four grafts) collecting a fifth: nothing is lost or duplicated.
- Dying with nothing carried leaves no remains and does not erase older ones. Dying again replaces them.
- A graft shed at the same moment as a death is not counted twice.
- A save with no `remains` section loads empty; a saved part id the data no longer holds is dropped, not a crash.
- A creature whose drop names an unknown part fails validation, not play.
