# Cocoons (E2d) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A spider webs a stunned living creature into a cocoon it carries (`prey_cocooned`), eats the cocoon later for the creature's essence, or lets the prey go (`prey_freed`), so the Broodmother, Cocoon-Mother and Anansi gates can be met.

**Architecture:** A cocoon is an inventory item with the id `cocoon:<creature>`, carried like any part (never worn, never spent as an offering or by a forge), at most two at once, kept in the remains. `Player.try_cocoon` is the spider's action on the `inspect` button; `try_release_prey` is its fallback when there is nothing to inspect, and the predate button eats a carried cocoon when there is nothing to eat. `Game` turns a released prey back into a stunned creature in the room.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`).

**Spec:** `docs/research/items-e2.md` (E2d), `docs/isekai-chronicles-design-doc.md` (a cocoon can hold living prey, webbed now and eaten later).

## Global Constraints

- Only the spider cocoons. Only a creature that is alive, stunned (`EnemyStatus.STUNNED`), awake and edible (`def.predatable`) can be cocooned; a corpse cannot.
- At most `Inventory.COCOON_COUNT_MAX` (2) cocoons are carried; a third is refused and costs nothing.
- A cocoon is not a part: it is never worn, never offered to a creature, never spent by a forge, and `Player.parts_carried()` does not count it.
- A cocooned creature leaves the room and is not downed: no `defeated`.
- Eating a cocoon is eating the creature: `predated`, its essences as `absorbed`, the heal, MP and XP of an eat.
- A freed creature is let out beside the spider, stunned for `Game.FREED_STUN_SECONDS`.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- A cocoon in the remains comes back through the usual rules (it is carried again, not worn, not lost) and a saved cocoon of a creature the data no longer holds is dropped.
- A third cocoon, a corpse, an active creature and the wrong species cocoon nothing and spend nothing.
- Eating or freeing with no cocoon carried does nothing and says nothing.
- A forge or a taming offer never spends a cocoon.
- A cocoon eaten whose creature is not in the player's catalog is refused, not lost.
