# The goblin's hands (E2c) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A goblin picks pockets, rigs traps, plants ward-stones and forges a weapon, and says `item_stolen`, `trap_sprung`, `ward_stone_placed` and `weapon_forged`, so the Leprechaun, Gremlin, Hearth God and Mastersmith gates can be met.

**Architecture:** A data verb spec may carry `deed` (said when it is cast, by a player only); the Tomte and Hearth God zones use it for ward-stones. Two new verb kinds: `trap` (a `VerbTrap` node an enemy springs) for the Tinker, and `forge` (spends carried parts for a lasting stat) for the Smith, with `VerbAbility.ready()` refusing a forge the player cannot pay for, at no cost. Pickpocket is a goblin action on the inspect button, after bonds.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), Python (`tools/form_verbs.json`, `tools/build_form_verbs.py`).

**Spec:** `docs/research/items-e2.md` (E2c), `docs/isekai-chronicles-design-doc.md` (goblin traps, forging, theft).

## Global Constraints

- A verb's `deed` is said only when the caster can say deeds (`emit_deed`); an enemy casting the same verb earns nothing.
- `trap` and `forge` are not enemy kinds: `VerbKinds.ENEMY_KINDS` stays as it is.
- A refused forge (fewer carried parts than it costs) spends no MP, no parts and starts no cooldown.
- A creature can be robbed once; only the goblin picks pockets; a worn graft is never spent or stolen.
- Traps hurt and stun enemies only, never the player, and at most `VerbKinds.TRAP_MAX` of a caster's stand at once.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- A forge with one part, a cooldown, or a full set of hands spends nothing it should not and does not double-spend.
- A trap sprung by a stunned, dormant or downed creature, or by the player, does nothing and says nothing.
- Robbing a creature with no drops, or twice, says nothing and takes nothing.
- A verb `deed` that names no known deed fails validation, not play.
- An enemy casting a ward-stone verb says no deed.
