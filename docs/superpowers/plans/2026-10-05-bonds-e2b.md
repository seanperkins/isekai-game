# Bonds and companions (E2b) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A wolf bonds with a stunned pack creature and a goblin tames a stunned creature with an offered part; the bonded creature follows, fights beside the player, and is lost for good if it dies. `bond_formed` is said with its kind, so the Warg, Lupa, Lycaon, Rat King, Wolf Lord and Beast Rider gates can be met.

**Architecture:** `CreatureDef.bonds` says which bonds a creature accepts. `Enemy.can_be_bonded()` says when. `Companion` is a node under the player's parent (so it survives room changes) with its own HP, strike and follow. `Player.try_bond()` is the species action on the `inspect` button (after interactables, before inspecting), and keeps the companion list and cap.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), Python generator `tools/build_creatures.py`.

**Spec:** `docs/research/items-e2.md` (the button rule and section E2b), `docs/isekai-chronicles-design-doc.md` (taming).

## Global Constraints

- Only a wolf (kind `pack`) or a goblin (kind `tame`, spending one carried part) bonds; the slime, undead and spider never do.
- Only a creature that is alive and stunned (`EnemyStatus.STUNNED`, not dormant) and lists the bond in `CreatureDef.bonds` can be bonded. A downed corpse cannot.
- One companion at a time; two for the form `goblin_lord_of_beasts`. A bond past the cap is refused and costs nothing.
- `bond_formed {kind, creature}` once per bond; a creature whose bonds list `large` says it again with `kind: large`; a bond that lasts `Companion.LONG_BOND_SECONDS` of play says it once more with `kind: long`.
- A companion that dies is gone for good. It is also gone with the player's death and with a new run.
- Taming teaches the goblin the creature's type (E2a's `learn_from`); a bond is not a kill: no `defeated`.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- A bond refused for the cap, a missing offering, a corpse, an active creature or the wrong species spends and removes nothing.
- A companion that outlives a room change keeps following; one whose owner dies is freed.
- Two bonds in one frame do not exceed the cap.
- A creature is bonded once: it leaves the room and cannot be bonded or eaten again.
- A saved game or data with an unknown bond name fails validation, not play.
