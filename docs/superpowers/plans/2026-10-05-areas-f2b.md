# Areas, part two: the swamp and the goblin village (F2b) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ten new rooms east of the forest, built by the F2a builder: a swamp (five rooms) and a goblin village (four rooms and the Sump), joined to the forest's Den and to the Flooded Tunnels' F6.

**Architecture:** Two area modules (`SwampArea` prefix `SW`, `VillageArea` prefix `VI`) registered after the forest. Two new kit helpers, `AreaKit.climb` (a zigzag of ledges up to a ceiling opening) and `AreaKit.flank` (a ledge either side of a floor opening), carry the vertical-exit pattern the forest wrote inline.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), `tools/build_areas.sh`.

**Spec:** `docs/research/areas-f2.md`, `docs/research/area-level-design.md` section 12.

## Global Constraints

- The builder never writes a world that has a `WorldValidator` error or a `RoomLint` finding; a re-run changes nothing (`tools/build_areas.sh --check` prints `0 rooms would change`).
- Shipped rooms change only by the exits the modules attach: FO9 gains a right exit, F6 gains a top exit labelled `swim`.
- Art is borrowed: swamp from the flooded tunnels (`flooded_*` decor), village from the cave (unprefixed decor). No new art.
- Spawns are creatures that exist now (support monsters that name the swamp or village in the design table); twins are placed in F2f.
- The village's gate shrine has a glow pool, no altar, and no spawns (VI1). No mud, trust, trial or tame mechanics: they are art and text until F2e.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- A climb up through a ceiling opening ends in the opening and every ledge of it passes `ledge_reach`.
- The village's neutral gatehouse (`VI1`) has no spawns and the Sump's exit to F6 carries `swim` on both ends.
- Without the new exits the shipped world is exactly what F2a left (the forest's tests still pass).
- Every swamp and village room is reachable from the start room, and the Flooded Tunnels are not reachable from the swamp without `swim`.
