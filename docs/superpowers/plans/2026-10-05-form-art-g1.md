# Art for the new forms (G1) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Every form with art is drawn with it: the 21 wolf, 21 spider, 28 goblin, 29 undead and 18 new slime forms use the stills the art track made (`assets/sheets/forms_<species>.png`) in the game (the body, the Tree and Form tabs), instead of a tinted base sheet.

**Architecture:** The art arrives as one sheet per species with one still frame per form, named by the form's slug (`moonbound` for `wolf_moonbound`; the slime's by its id). `FormDef` gains `sprite_frame` beside `sprite_set`; the form generator fills both for every form whose frame exists in the sheet; the validator checks the frame; `Player` draws a form with a still frame as that frame (turned to a surface for the spider) and takes its hurt shape from it. The older animated form sheets (`form_weaver` and so on) keep their clips.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT, Python 3 (the form generator).

**Spec:** `docs/research/species-form-branches.md` section 9 and the art specs `tools/art/forms_*_frames.json`.

## Global Constraints

- Baseline 2825 tests on `main` (20c9c1e). The sheets, the frame specs and the bestiary page data (`python3 tools/build_bestiary.py`) are committed; the raw frames (`art_source/frames/forms_*`, about 160 MB) stay on disk and are git-ignored, as the terrain's raw images are.
- A frame name is the form's id without its species prefix (`wolf_moonbound` to `moonbound`); a slime form's is its id. A form with no frame in its species' sheet keeps `sprite_set ""`.
- Art stills are drawn at the sheet's own size (`body_scale()` 1), facing right and mirrored for left. A form with no `sprite_frame` draws exactly as before.
- The undead base (the skeleton) and the goblin, wolf, spider and slime bases keep their animated look; their stage-2+ forms use stills.

## Review Focus

- A form whose sheet or frame is missing falls back to the tinted base look, never an error. (Tasks 1, 2)
- Evolving between a still form and an animated one (a slime Weaver to a still Gleipnir branch, an animated base to a still wolf) swaps the look both ways. (Task 2)
- The hurt shape follows the still frame and its mirror. (Task 2)
- A spider still on a wall is turned to it, and its thread still draws. (Task 2)
