# Lessons (E2a) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use ml:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** The goblin learns instead of devouring: its first dealing with each creature type in a life is a lesson that pays that creature's essence once and says `lesson_learned`; repeats pay a trickle; eating only heals. The Tinker's "lesson from junk" gets a creature to learn from.

**Architecture:** `Lessons` is a pure per-life record (`learn(def)` says what a dealing pays). `Player.learn_from(enemy)` applies it for the goblin when a creature is downed or eaten, and `_complete_predation` stops paying a goblin essence. `Enemy.lesson_paid` makes a creature pay once however it ends. `junk` is a generated support monster.

**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.7 GDScript, GUT (`tools/run_tests.sh [filter]`), Python generators (`uv run --with Pillow python tools/build_creatures.py`).

**Spec:** `docs/research/items-e2.md` (section E2a), `docs/isekai-chronicles-design-doc.md` (goblin essence method: lessons per creature type).

## Global Constraints

- Only the goblin learns; every other species eats and downs exactly as before.
- A lesson says `lesson_learned {creature, species, element}` (`species` and `element` omitted when empty); `Deeds.TAGS` already allows those three tags.
- A creature pays once: downed then eaten, or eaten alive, is one dealing.
- A repeat with the same creature id pays one unit of its largest essence and says no deed.
- Lessons are per life: a new run empties them.
- No push. No attribution lines in commits. No time or effort estimates.

## Review Focus

- A goblin that downs a creature and then eats its corpse is paid once, and says one lesson.
- A second creature of an already-known type pays a trickle of one and says no `lesson_learned`.
- A goblin eating a water pool (no creature) heals and crashes nothing.
- Eating as a wolf, a spider, an undead or a slime pays its essence as before and says no lesson.
- A creature with no essences still teaches (the deed, no `element` tag) and pays nothing.
