# Skill Tree Screen — Design

Status: draft for Sean's review, revised after the first debate round (2026-10-03). The panel's five reviews are in
`docs/ledgers/spec-review-2026-10-03/round-1/`. Built from the design doc (`docs/isekai-chronicles-design-doc.md`): "one combined,
zoomable graph … if evolutions are hard to show alongside powers, fall back to one evolution at a time, plus where the next
evolution is once unlocked". It is built **after** the essence overhaul (`2026-10-03-essence-overhaul-design.md`), whose
`FormDef.powers`, `FormOffers.open_lineages`, `evolution_price` and `held` it reads.

## What I understood

Today the Skills tab is a grouped list (Proficiency, Essence, Evolution) and the Compendium tab is a second list of discovery
states. Neither shows how powers connect to each other or to the body forms they lead to. Sean wants a **node graph**: connected
nodes you can zoom out to see the shape and zoom in to read, that **grows as you find it**.

I read "evolutions alongside powers" as **body forms alongside powers**: the graph holds the powers (with their evolution branches,
which are skills) and the form stages (slime, then lineage, then child, then sovereign). The fallback then means: if forms and
powers will not fit legibly at 640×360, show **one lineage of forms at a time** (the current body and its next stage). If that
reading is wrong, see Open for Sean 1.

Success:

- a **Tree** tab opens a graph of every discovered power and form, with `???` stubs one step past what you know;
- you can select, zoom and read a node on **keyboard and a pad**;
- forms you reached in earlier lives stay on the graph, so the tree is a record of the soul, not of one life;
- a recipe's exact ingredients show only for a power you have owned once (the Compendium's rule), never before;
- a secret skill leaves no trace on the graph until you own it;
- the labels are legible at 640×360 at the normal zoom (checked by screenshot, below).

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Where | A new **Tree** tab, second in the strip. The Skills tab stays: it is where actives are assigned to slots, which a graph is poor at. The Compendium and Form tabs stay as they are, and the Form tab is still where the body evolution is **chosen**; the tree shows forms read-only. **The strip gets one constant layout.** Adding `"tree"` makes `TABS.size()` six, so the existing `tab_layout(n)` branch (`n <= TABS.size()`) would own the common six-tab strip at the five-tab stride and end it at x = 634 on the 640 px canvas; the Form tab (shown only at the level cap or after a body evolution) makes seven. Base tabs can no longer drop below six, so the two-stride switch has no job left: one stride of 84 and one width of 80 serve every size we can reach (six tabs end at 528, seven at 612, inside the 28 to 612 margin the screen already uses), and `TAB_STRIDE_SIX`, `TAB_W_SIX` and the branch go. Whether "COMPENDIUM" (ten glyphs at `FONT_MAIN`) fits 80 px is unknown and is settled by screenshot. The index shift touches the tests listed below |
| Model | A pure `SkillTreeModel` (like `SkillScreenModel.map_view`): `build(rules, compendium, forms, reached_forms, current_form)` returns nodes, edges and a layout; no scene code, so everything but the drawing is unit-testable |
| Nodes | `power` (a non-enemy skill that is not an evolution), `evolution` (a skill whose `source` is `evolution`), `form` (a `FormDef`, plus the base slime as the stage-1 root). Each node carries a state: `owned`, `named` (known but not yet owned, the common case), `retired` (evolved away: dim, "evolved"), `closed` (a sibling took the branch: dim), `ready` (an evolution whose conditions are met: glows, with an `affordable` flag so the card can say what is short), `current` (the body's form), `reached`, or `stub`. The stage-1 slime is **reached by definition** in the model; it is never stored |
| Edges | power → evolution (the evolution's `replaces`); power → the stage-2 form of every lineage that lists it in `FormDef.powers` ("opens"); form → child form (`parents`); slime → each stage-2 form. Nothing else connects. **Every edge's two endpoints are visible nodes**: a filter that removes a node removes every edge touching it before the layout is returned |
| Growing | A **skill** node shows when its Compendium state is at least Named (a Hinted node also shows its hint; Owned-once also shows its conditions, with the elements named as the card does). An **Unknown** skill is absent, except that an evolution of a power you have **owned once** (Compendium Owned-once, as the doc says: stubs for the branches of powers you own) appears as a `???` stub until it is Named; a power that is merely Named does not reveal its branches. A **secret** skill (`SkillDef.secret`, for example Glutton) is absent, and its layout slot is not reserved, until you own it. The Skills tab keeps secrets out of every `???` teaser (the Compendium tab does not filter them, so Glutton already shows there as `???`); the tree follows the Skills tab, because an empty reserved slot is a clue and the tree has no reason to give one. A **stage-2 form** is shown whenever any power that opens it is shown, so an "opens" edge never dangles; it is **named** when it has been reached or its lineage is open this life (`FormOffers.open_lineages`, the same function that decides the first offers), otherwise a stub. A **stage-3 or stage-4 form** is a stub once its parent is named, and named only when reached: it is not named merely because its lineage is open. The **Greater lineage** (four forms) has no opening power, so none of the rules above reach it: its stage-2 form, Greater Slime, is **named** when it has been reached or the Form tab is offering it now (`open_lineages(...).size() < 2`), and is otherwise a stub that is always present under the slime; its children and sovereign follow the stage-3 and stage-4 rule. A stub never shows a name, an icon or ingredients |
| Layout | Two bands in one canvas. **Upper:** powers. The four that evolve are drawn as three-node trees (base, then its two branches to the right) and, with Echolocation, Body Armor, Hardened Shell and Tremor (the other powers that open a lineage), are **ordered by the lineage they open** (`FormOffers.LINEAGE_ORDER`) so the "opens" edges converge on adjacent rows instead of crossing an alphabetical list; the other eleven powers (Appraisal, Glutton, Jolt, Leap, Mana Recovery, Pain Resistance, Poison Resistance, Regeneration, Swim, Toughness, Wall Cling) have no edges at all and are packed in a grid of columns, not one per row (nineteen base powers, one per row, would stand about 300 to 370 px tall against the 274 px list). **Lower:** forms, the stage across (slime, stage 2, 3, 4) and the lineages down. The "opens" edges run from a power in the upper band to its lineage's stage-2 form in the lower. Positions are computed from the **full** tree minus secret skills (columns by depth; rows by lineage order for the edge-bearing powers, name order for the grid, and `FormOffers.LINEAGE_ORDER` for the form lineages), so a node never moves when another is discovered, and an undiscovered node leaves its slot empty, which also shows how much is left to find. **Secret skills are the one exception to "never moves":** they take cells in the grid's trailing row, in id order, and only that row may change when one is first owned (today there is one, Glutton, so nothing moves); a reserved empty cell would give the secret away This is the Map tab's rule (`map_view` returns the bounds of the whole world so its scale never shifts). The plan computes the canvas from the node size; the target is a canvas no taller than two screens at the normal zoom, with the camera doing the rest |
| Zoom and pan | Two zoom levels: **overview** (dots and edges, no labels, to see the shape) and **normal** (name and state), flipped by **one** `tree_zoom` action (a third level and a second action arrive together, when a third level exists). The detail a third level would add is already in the card beside the graph. The camera is a pure function of the selected node and the zoom level, so it **follows the selection**; there is no free pan and **no mouse in v1** (nothing under `scripts/ui` handles the mouse today). The **direction keys and the D-pad** move the selection with `SkillTreeModel.neighbor(id, dir)`. The left stick is **not** wired in v1: `nav_step` takes the y axis only, `_process` passes only `Controls.last_stick.y`, and `_unhandled_input` swallows every `InputEventJoypadMotion`, so two-axis stick navigation would change `nav_step`'s signature (pinned by `tests/test_menu_input.gd`); it is a follow-up |
| Navigation | `neighbor(id, dir)` prefers the nearest node **joined by an edge** in that direction, else the nearest node by distance in that half-plane, ties broken by node id, and returns the same id when nothing lies that way. A test builds the whole graph and walks it with the same relation: every visible node must be reachable from the root by repeated moves, so a layout that strands a node fails |
| Input | Actions are registered at runtime in `autoload/controls.gd` (`BINDINGS`, `PAD_BUTTONS`, `PAD_AXES`, `ensure_actions`), **not** in `project.godot`, which has no `[input]` section. The one new action, `tree_zoom`, goes there, and it needs a **keyboard binding**: `ensure_actions` iterates `BINDINGS` and reads `PAD_BUTTONS` inside that loop, so an action with only a stick click is never registered. The pad triggers are `active_3` and `active_4`, so they are not free; the stick clicks (`JOY_BUTTON_LEFT_STICK`, `JOY_BUTTON_RIGHT_STICK`) are unmapped and one of them is the pad binding. Taken on the keyboard: Q and E (tabs, and the slot aliases for `active_3` and `active_4`), W A S D, the arrows, Enter and Space, Backspace and Esc, J and Shift, K and F, I and R, U, O, H, L, F3 and F11; minus is unbound and is a candidate. The screen already consumes joypad motion while open; the tree handler consumes its own events the same way. The hint line switches with `Controls.using_joypad` as the other tabs do |
| Detail | The existing right-hand card shows the selected node. A power reuses `SkillScreenModel.detail`; an evolution adds its price and what you hold ("Evolve for water 6, dark 10 — you hold 8 water, 4 dark"); a form shows its stats, grants and blurb and says whether it is current, reached earlier or unreached. The Form tab's card is built imperatively inside `SkillScreen._build_form`, not in the model, and it hides the grants whose skills have retired, so reusing it means extracting a model function `form_card(def, rules)` that both read (the rules are needed to keep that behaviour). Hidden conditions follow `compendium_rows`: shown only at Owned-once, with `condition_text` (which gains the `source` wording from the essence overhaul) |
| Evolving | **Read-only in v1.** A ready evolution's card says what is short or "Evolve in the Skills tab". The Skills tab stays the one place a power is evolved, so no second entry point, no shared helper and no second arm state to disarm across tabs (`_armed`, reset in `close()`). Open for Sean 3 asks whether to add it |
| Persistence | A list of reached form ids, `forms_reached`, on **`WorldProgress`**, which already holds what the player has found (visited rooms, shortcuts, tablets, rebirths) through `Profile.list_section`. `list_section` validates that every element is a string and drops a malformed section with a warning, so nested validation is not needed. There is no species key (form ids are unique, and a new section needs no migration), no new class and no new file. It is written from `Player.advance_form`, the one place a body evolution succeeds, through a new `Player` signal (`form_advanced(id)`) that `Game` connects to `progress.reach_form`. The `evolved_body` event is **not** used: `EventBus.world_event` is documented as audio-only ("Never counted; only Audio listens"). An editor Play builds a fresh in-memory `WorldProgress`, so the connection gives it the isolation it already has: its evolutions never reach the real profile. `WorldProgress._save` saves through the same `Profile` the Compendium uses (`autoload/compendium.gd`), so a failed write is already warned there. The stage-1 slime is always reached. The Compendium stays global for now; splitting it per species belongs to the spider spec |
| Fallback | If the combined graph fails the legibility bar below at the normal zoom, the plan adds a `forms_focus` option to `build()` that keeps only the current form, its parent line and its next stage (with its stubs). It removes the nodes it drops **and every edge that touched them**, including the "opens" edges from the power band, so no edge dangles. The power band is unchanged. This is Sean's "one evolution at a time", and it is a model filter, so it needs no second view. It is a **conditional step** of the plan, built only if `tools/tree_shots.gd` shows the bar failing, so it is not built and tested speculatively |

## Rendering

The screen is a code-built `CanvasLayer` (layer 20) at 640×360. The Map tab is the precedent for a drawn view: pure data in `SkillScreenModel`, drawn into a box with panels and `ColorRect`s, rebuilt on every `_refresh` (it calls `_clear` and builds again). The tree follows that idiom: with the camera a pure function of the selected node and the zoom level, the only state that must outlive a rebuild is the selected id and one zoom flag, which sit on `SkillScreen` beside `_sel` and `_scroll` (`switch_tab` already resets them). The drawing lives in a new `SkillTreeView` (its own file: a builder that fills a clipped `Control` with node panels and one `_draw` pass for the edges, `COL_BORDER` for a known edge and `COL_DIM` for an edge to a stub), so `skill_screen.gd` (already 789 lines) does not grow a second, unrelated responsibility. Node count is bounded (27 non-enemy skills, which are 19 base powers and 8 evolutions, minus the secret ones until owned, plus 25 form nodes, which are the 24 forms and the slime root, and fewer while undiscovered), so no culling is needed. Labels use `FONT_SMALL` (8) at the normal zoom; overview hides them.

**Legibility bar** (what the screenshot check decides): at the normal zoom with everything discovered, no two nodes overlap, no edge
runs through a node's label, every label fits its node untruncated, the selected node's card is readable beside the graph, and the
selected node and its edge neighbours fit on one screen with the camera following the selection.

## Engine and data changes

- `scripts/ui/skill_tree_model.gd` (new): nodes, edges, states, layout, `neighbor`, the `forms_focus` filter.
- `scripts/ui/skill_tree_view.gd` (new): the builder (nodes, the camera as a function of selection and zoom, the edge pass).
- `scripts/world/world_progress.gd`: `forms_reached`, `reach_form(id)`, `is_form_reached(id)`.
- `scripts/player/player.gd`: `signal form_advanced(id)`, emitted from `advance_form`; `scripts/game.gd` connects it to `progress.reach_form`.
- `scripts/ui/skill_screen.gd`: the Tree tab hosts the view and keeps the selected id and zoom flag; the constant tab strip (the numbers above; `TAB_STRIDE_SIX`, `TAB_W_SIX` and the `tab_layout` branch go); the `tree_zoom` key.
- `scripts/ui/skill_screen_model.gd`: the node card text (price, held, `form_card(def, rules)`).
- `autoload/controls.gd`: the `tree_zoom` action (a key and a stick click).
- `tools/tree_shots.gd` (new, modelled on `tools/evolution_shots.gd`): screenshots of the tab at each zoom with nothing, a little and everything discovered.

## Sequencing

Two steps, plus one conditional, so Open for Sean 1 gates only the second and nothing is wasted if the answer is "power branches only":

1. **The power graph:** the model for powers and their evolutions, the view, the tab, the strip, `tree_zoom`, `tools/tree_shots.gd`. It needs none of the form work, so it can start without the essence overhaul's step 1.
2. **The forms band:** `forms_reached` and the `form_advanced` signal, `form_card`, the stage-2 "opens" edges, form stubs and the Greater rule.
3. *(conditional)* `forms_focus`, only if the shot check fails.

## Testing

Run `tools/run_tests.sh`.

- **Model:** every non-enemy, non-secret skill and every form appears as a node when fully discovered; every evolution has exactly one parent edge, and each stage-2 form has one "opens" edge per power it lists; **every edge endpoint is a visible node**, with and without `forms_focus`; an undiscovered skill is absent and its discovered parent shows it as a stub; a stub carries no name; Owned-once is the only state with ingredients; a secret skill is absent and reserves no slot until owned; a Named-but-locked power is `named` and a ready evolution carries `affordable`; a stage-2 form whose lineage is open but unreached is named, and its stage-3 children are stubs; Greater Slime is named when reached or offered and a stub otherwise; retired and closed derive from the engine as the Skills tab does; layout is deterministic and computed from the full tree, nodes never overlap, and discovering a non-secret node never moves another (a revealed secret changes only the grid's trailing row); walking `neighbor` from the root reaches every visible node; the `forms_focus` filter keeps the current form, its parents and its next stage only.
- **Persistence:** the slime is reached by definition and never stored; `forms_reached` round trip through `Profile`; a malformed section is dropped with a warning; `reached` survives a new life; an editor Play does not write to the real profile; the signal fires on a successful `advance_form` and not on a refused one.
- **Screen:** the Tree tab is second; selection follows `neighbor`; `tree_zoom` flips between the two levels; the camera is the same for the same (selection, zoom) after a rebuild, and keeps the selection inside the box; every strip size we can reach (six and seven tabs) ends at or before x = 612 (stated as numbers); the hint line switches with the scheme; the `tree_zoom` action exists after `Controls.ensure_actions` (a key is bound).
- **Changed by the tab insertion (listed so the plan does not discover them):** `test_skill_screen` (the numeric tab indices), `test_audio_ui` (indices and `TABS.size() == 5`), `test_map` (indices), `test_form_tab` (the Form tab at index 5, `tab_layout(5)` as "today's layout"), `test_evolution_screen` (indices), and `tools/evolution_shots.gd` (`switch_tab(5)`).
- **By eye:** `tools/tree_shots.gd` at 640×360 for each zoom with nothing, partly and fully discovered, judged against the legibility bar; the result decides whether `forms_focus` ships on and whether "COMPENDIUM" fits the strip.

## Not here

- Mouse selection, pan and zoom.
- A third (close) zoom level, free pan, and the left stick moving the selection (it needs a two-axis `nav_step`).
- Evolving from the tree (Open for Sean 3).
- Per-species Compendium and shared-skill display (the open question about a power two species share): it arrives with the spider.
- Choosing the body evolution in the tree. The Form tab keeps that.
- Retiring the Compendium tab, which this graph makes partly redundant (Open for Sean 2).
- Stage 2 → 3 pairing by the evolved power (the tree draws today's "both children" edges until the pairings are authored; see the essence overhaul spec).
- A free-form node editor, tooltips on hover, search.

## Open for Sean

1. **Do "evolutions" mean body forms?** I built the combined graph from powers plus forms. If you meant only the **power** evolution branches, the graph is smaller and the fallback is unneeded; the forms stay on the Form tab. Say so and I drop step 2 of the sequence: the lower band, `forms_reached`, the `form_advanced` signal, `form_card` and the form edges.
2. **The tabs.** Recommended: insert Tree second and keep Skills, Compendium and Form beside it, because the Skills tab assigns slots and the Compendium holds hints and conditions in a form that reads well as a list. The cost is three views of overlapping information and a seven-tab strip. Two alternatives from the review: **replace the Compendium tab with the Tree** (both read the same Compendium state, the base stays five tabs, and `tab_layout` and the index tests stay as they are), or **append Tree at the end** (no index shift, an odd order).
3. **Evolve from the tree.** v1 is read-only. Adding it brings a second entry point, a shared helper with the Skills tab, and an arm state to keep in step. Say so if you want it.
4. **Mouse.** Left out of v1. Say so if you want click-to-select and wheel zoom on desktop.
