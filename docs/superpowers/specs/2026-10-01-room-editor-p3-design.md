# Room Editor, Phase 3 — Design

Status: draft (2026-10-01). Phase 3 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-room-editor-design.md`, after P2
(`2026-09-30-room-editor-p2-design.md`, shipped). Sean's queue is: finish the editor, then add biomes and enemies from *So I'm a
Spider, So What?* and *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*. The art for two new biomes (`deep`, `flooded`: terrain, back
walls, parallax layers, decor sprites) is already in `assets/`, but no room uses them and the editor cannot make one: its area
list is the constant `["cave", "grotto"]`, it has no way to place decor, and it cannot move the world's start or delete a room.
P3 is the phase that makes the editor able to author those rooms. Research: `docs/research/level-design-reference.md`.

## What I understood

After P2 Sean can build a room's solids, creatures, exits, features and kits without a text editor. He cannot yet: choose a
room's biome from what the art supports; place the decor that makes a room read as its biome (crystals, fungus, hanging roots);
move or resize a thing to an exact number; resize a ledge by dragging its edge; turn a thin ledge into one you cannot jump up
through; copy a thing to another room; pick the right one of two overlapping things; make a room smaller or delete it; set the
world's start; give an exit its `gate`, a tablet its `hint`, a pool its room area and affinity; or play with any kit but Wall
Cling. He also cannot see the jump budget he is building to, or check the world from CI.

**Success for P3:** author a complete room in a *new biome* without a text editor: pick `deep` or `flooded`, draw it, dress it
with that biome's decor (standing and hanging), copy a ledge or a creature from another room, fix a ledge to exact numbers,
delete a room made by mistake, make the room the world's start, and play it with a kit of his choosing. "Validate clean" stays
the same thing as "the suite's rule tests would pass", for decor and the start as well.

## The phases

| Phase | What it delivers |
|---|---|
| P1 (shipped) | Separate editor scene; Solids, creatures, paired exits, New room beside, undo, Validate, save, Play from here |
| P2 (shipped) | `RoomLint`, the live problems list, features (pool, tablet, switch, rebirth pool), the inspector (exit shortcut, tablet text, switch, pool kit), Grow, two Play toggles, the World view |
| **P3 (this spec)** | Biomes from data and a Room panel (area, delete); Decor tool; the Start tool; resize handles and numeric fields; the hard-ledge toggle; copy, cut, paste, duplicate; selection cycling; shrinking and deleting rooms; exit `gate`, tablet `hint`, a pool's area and affinity; a free-form Play kit; a jump ruler and a collision-only view; a JSON lint export |
| P4 (later spec) | Physical reachability from one shared surface model, per-room assumed abilities, `gate`-aware lint and the stricter `over_hole`; prefab stamps and room templates; moving a room on the World view; dressing (see Ruling 1); autosave |

## Decisions (P3)

| Area | Decision |
|---|---|
| Biomes | The area list comes from data, not a constant: `TerrainArt.biomes() -> Array` returns the sorted directory names under `res://assets/tiles/` that `has_biome` accepts (today `cave`, `deep`, `flooded`, `grotto`). New Room's Area control and the Room panel read it; adding a biome's art makes it appear with no code change. A room whose `area` has no terrain art is not refused (the game falls back to flat tiles); Validate does not change. |
| The Room panel | The right-hand slot, when **nothing is selected** and no problems list is open, shows the room panel: id (read-only), size in screens (read-only; Grow and Shrink change it), an **Area** dropdown (`TerrainArt.biomes()`), and a **Delete room** button. Changing the area is one undo step and sets `area` only; it does not touch decor, dressing or pools. If the room has `dressing` and the new biome has no piece library, the validator already reports it (`dressing needs a piece library`), which is the prompt to delete it. |
| Selection kinds | Three new kinds beside `solid`, `spawn`, `exit`, `feature`: **`decor`**, **`start`** (index 0: a room has at most one) and the inspector reads them. `hit` priority: spawn, feature, start, decor, exit, solid. Every kind has an explicit arm in `hit`, `begin_move`, `move_to`, `delete_selection`, the outline and the inspector, and each `_` arm refuses (the rule P2 set). `delete_selection` on `start` is refused ("a world needs a start: use the Start tool to move it"). |
| Selection cycling | The model gains `hit_all(room_id, p, pick) -> Array` (every element under the point, in the priority order above, smaller solids first); `hit` is its first element. A **click without a drag** on the already-selected element, with more than one candidate under the point, selects the next candidate (wrapping). A drag, or a click on an unselected element, behaves as in P2. The view records the candidates at press and decides at release (`serial` unchanged = a click). |
| Decor | New `DecorLib` (`scripts/world/decor_lib.gd`): a const `CATALOG` of `id -> {biome, anchor, light}` for every decor sprite in `assets/sprites/` (the crystals, fungus, hanging roots, stalactites and so on; creature, icon, vfx and tile sprites are not decor), `ids_for(biome) -> Array`, `entry(id, pos) -> Dictionary` (the data a placed piece gets: `anchor: "top"` only when the catalog says so, `light` only when it has one) and `texture_box(id)`. Catalog defaults are pinned against the shipped rooms: every shipped decor entry equals `entry(id, pos)` for its id (anchor and light), so the table is the data's own convention. A decor id is bound to a biome by its prefix (`grotto_`, `deep_`, `flooded_`; none = cave). The **Decor tool** shows the palette for the current room's area; a click places a piece that **stands on the surface below the click** (`anchor` bottom: `surface_below`) or **hangs from the rock above it** (`anchor` top: new `surface_above`, the underside of the first solid or the ceiling at or above the click), refused with a message where there is none. Select drags it with the same rule. A decor id outside the catalog is a lint finding (`decor_unknown`, thirteenth rule). Decor is not constrained by `pool_clearance` or `in_rock`; `over_hole` already covers it. |
| The Start tool | One click on open floor sets the **world's** start: `RoomEditModel.set_start(room_id, x) -> String`. The start's y is always the room's floor stand (`height - FLOOR - BodyConfig.BOTTOM`, the `start_floor` rule), x is snapped and refused in a wall column or rock. Setting it in a room that is not the start room moves the world start: the old room's `start` becomes `NO_START`, **and the default rebirth pool (`WorldProgress.DEFAULT_POOL`, feature id `C1`) moves with it**, because the validator requires that pool in the start room. The pool is placed 80 px beside the start on the floor (right, else left), keeps its id, area and kit, and the whole move is one undo step across up to three rooms; if the pool cannot be placed (no valid floor 80 px either side) the move is refused with that reason and nothing changes. With no default pool in the world (deleted by hand) only the start moves and the validator's own message stands. The start also drags along the floor with Select (x only) and has an inspector (x). |
| Resize | A selected solid draws eight handles (corners and edge midpoints, a fixed 5 screen px, picked within `pick`). Dragging one resizes the rect on its side(s) in 4 px steps, never below `MIN_SOLID`, clipped to the room, never flipping past the opposite side. One undo step; `hard_ledges` follows the rect exactly as it does for a move. Handles belong to solids only. |
| Numeric fields | The inspector gains a number control (a `SpinBox`, whole pixels, commits on Enter or focus loss exactly like a `LineEdit`) and these `get_field` / `set_field` keys, each validated as a drag to the same place would be and refused with a reason, changing nothing: solid `x`, `y`, `w`, `h`, `hard`; creature `x`, `y`, `creature` (an id from the palette); feature `x` (its base y is the surface and is shown, not edited); decor `x`, `y`; start `x`; exit `from`, `to` (both halves, by the same rules as `add_exit`). Typed values are not snapped to the grid: the point of a typed number is precision. `EditorPanels.typing()` already treats a `SpinBox` as typing. |
| Hard ledges | A solid that is thin (`RoomBuilder.is_one_way` before the mark) gets a **Rock from below** checkbox, the `hard` field: it adds or removes the rect in `hard_ledges`. A solid that stops being thin (resized or typed) **loses its mark** in the same step, because a thick solid is rock anyway and the validator rejects a mark on one. The ledge's live label (`one-way ledge` / `rock`) reads the mark. |
| Clipboard | `RoomEditModel.clipboard` (a deep copy, held by the model so it survives room switches and a Play round trip): **Cmd/Ctrl+C** copies the selection, **X** cuts, **V** pastes, **D** duplicates (copy and paste 16 px down and right). Copyable: solid, creature, feature, decor. Not copyable: exit, start (refused with a reason). Pasting needs a current room and places at the pointer when it is inside the room, else 16 px from the original; the paste is validated as a placement of that kind (`add_solid` clipping, `add_spawn`, `add_feature`'s surface rule, decor's anchor rule), so a paste in rock is refused rather than landing in it. Pasted features get fresh generated ids; a pasted tablet keeps its text, a pasted pool its kit; **the default pool is not copyable** (its id is the world's). Cut is copy plus delete in one undo step; the clipboard keeps the element, so a paste refused in rock loses nothing. One element at a time (no multi-select). |
| Shrinking | `RoomEditModel.shrink_room(room_id, side, screens := 1) -> String`, sides `left`, `right`, `top` (bottom anchored, as Grow). It is Grow's inverse: left or top shift every local position back (`start` only when `is_start()`, this room's exits along the shrunk axis). It is **refused, changing nothing, when anything sits in the strip being removed**: a solid, creature, feature, decor piece, `dressing` entry or the start that overlaps it, or an exit on that side; the message counts what is in the way and names the first (`3 things are in the strip: move or delete them first (first: solid 40..80, y 100..116)`). A room never shrinks below one screen. One undo step. |
| Deleting a room | `RoomEditModel.delete_room(room_id) -> String`, from the Room panel (a two-click confirm: the button turns into "Really delete C7?" for a few seconds, no modal dialog) and refused for the start room ("move the start first"). It removes the room, **removes the partner half of every exit that led to it** in the neighbours (so no neighbour is left pointing at nothing; an exit with a `shortcut` goes with its partner, and the switch it left behind in another room is then lint's `shortcut_pair`), and is one undo step across every room it touched. The editor opens the room you came from. **Save removes the file** (`<save_dir>/<id>.tres`) of a deleted room: P1's rule that a room that was on disk when the editor opened is never deleted is retired for rooms the author explicitly deleted, and the status line says `removed the deleted room C7`. Git is the safety net, as for every save. Undo before Save cancels the removal; Undo after Save recreates the room as a dirty room and the next Save writes it again. |
| Exit `gate` | An exit's inspector gains **Gate**: a dropdown of `none` and the kit-legal skill ids (`wall_cling` is the one shipped). It sets `gate` on both halves in one step (the same pairing as `shortcut`) and removes it for `none`. A `gate` is a label for the validator and lint (a gated exit does not count in the ungated route; `over_hole` skips it); it does not stop the player in play. Its physical enforcement is P4. |
| Tablet `hint` | The tablet inspector gains **Hint**: `none` plus `RoomLint.hintable_skill_ids()`. `none` removes the key. `hint_unknown` still guards hand-edited data. |
| Pool area and affinity | The pool inspector gains **Area** (the biome list) and an **Affinity** block: one number control per essence in `RebirthKit.ESSENCES` (0 removes the entry), merged into `kit.affinity` so no other kit key is touched. The existing `RebirthKit.validate` stays the only judge; the eligibility rule (a pool must leave at least two evolution lineages) is the validator's, so an affinity that breaks it shows in Validate, not as a refusal. |
| Play kit | The Wall Cling toggle is replaced by **Kit...**, which opens a panel in the right-hand slot with the same controls as a pool's kit (starting level, the kit-legal skill checklist) bound to `play_options["kit"]`, plus a **Cave movement** button that ticks Leap and Wall Cling. `play_options` becomes `{"kit": Dictionary, "shortcuts": bool}` and rides in `Game.editor_resume`; `Game.play_request["kit"]` is that dictionary (affinity is not offered: it only matters at the first evolution). The kit control is one shared `KitEditor` used by the pool inspector and this panel. Open shortcuts stays a toggle. |
| Jump ruler | A **Ruler** toggle draws, around the pointer, the base jump's reach in room pixels from `RoomLint.REACH_RISE`, `REACH_GAP` and `REACH_HOP` (the same constants the `ledge_reach` rule uses): the rise box, the gap and the hop, as outlines with their numbers. It models only the base jump (no Wall Cling, swing or dash), and says so in its label. |
| Collision view | A **Collision** toggle hides the built art and draws every solid as a flat rectangle (rock one colour, one-way ledges another, hard ledges outlined) with gates, so geometry can be judged without the tiles. It changes nothing in the data. |
| JSON export | `RoomLint.to_json(rooms, creature_ids) -> String` (findings and validator strings, each `{room, rule, text}`), and `tools/lint_rooms.gd` / `tools/lint_rooms.sh`, which load `res://data/rooms`, print that JSON and exit non-zero when it is not empty. For CI and scripts; the editor does not call it. |

## Architecture (P3)

- **Model** (`room_edit_model.gd`): kinds `decor` and `start`; `hit_all`; `set_start`, `shrink_room`, `delete_room` (a `_deleted_on_save` set beside `_removed_on_save`, written by `save_dirty`); `clipboard`, `copy_selection`, `cut_selection`, `paste`, `duplicate_selection`; `resize(handle, delta)` reusing `_drag`; `surface_above`; new `get_field` / `set_field` keys. The file is already about 900 lines: the clipboard and the room lifecycle (`shrink_room`, `delete_room`, `set_start`) move to **`room_edit_ops.gd`**, a `RefCounted` that works on the model's `rooms`, `_snap` and `_push` (it is constructed with the model), so the model keeps the selection, undo and save. This is the split P2's Ruling 13 deferred.
- **Lint** (`room_lint.gd`): the thirteenth rule `decor_unknown`; `to_json`.
- **World data** (`decor_lib.gd`, `terrain_art.gd`): `DecorLib`, `TerrainArt.biomes()`.
- **View** (`room_view.gd`): tools `decor` and `start`; handles; marker boxes for decor and the start; the ruler and collision overlays; click-cycle bookkeeping.
- **Panels** (`editor_panels.gd`): tools Decor and Start in row one (Select, Solid, Creature, Feature, Decor, Start, Exit); a decor palette for the Decor tool; row two gains Ruler, Collision and Kit... and loses Wall Cling; New Room's Area control from `TerrainArt.biomes()`.
- **Inspector and slot** (`inspector_panel.gd`, `editor_slot.gd`, new `kit_editor.gd`, new `room_panel.gd`): the number control, the dropdown control, the per-kind field table extended to `solid`, `spawn`, `decor`, `start` and the new feature and exit keys; the slot shows the room panel when nothing is selected.
- **Game** (`game.gd`): `play_request["kit"]` is already accepted; no change beyond what P2 did. `play_options` is the editor's.
- **Docs and tools**: `docs/rooms.md` (decor catalog, the start and its pool), `docs/playtest-checklist.md`, `tools/editor_shots.gd`, `tools/lint_rooms.gd`.

## Failure modes and edge cases

- **Resize.** A handle dragged past the opposite side clamps at `MIN_SOLID`, never flips; a resize that would leave the room is clipped; resizing a hard ledge to thick drops its mark in the same step; a resize that makes a solid overlap a creature, feature or exit is allowed (lint reports `in_rock` / `exit_blocked`), as a drag of a solid already is.
- **Numeric fields.** An out-of-range or non-number string reverts the control and reports the reason; typing the stored value pushes no step (`_push` declines an unchanged room); a creature's `y` in rock or a feature's `x` over a hole is refused like a drag there; a `from` / `to` that breaks the exit rules is refused with `add_exit`'s reason; `from > to` is swapped, as `add_exit` swaps.
- **Cycling.** With one candidate a click on the selected element keeps it selected; a click on empty space clears the selection as before; a drag never cycles.
- **Clipboard.** Pasting with an empty clipboard says so; a pasted solid is clipped to the room and refused when it ends below `MIN_SOLID`; a pasted decor piece obeys its anchor rule at the paste point; pasting across a biome boundary is allowed (decor of another biome in a room is the author's choice, see Ruling 5); cutting the last tablet or switch leaves a lint finding if its partner remains (`shortcut_pair`); copying the default pool is refused; pasted ids never collide (`_feature_id` picks the first unused).
- **Decor.** A hanging piece finds the generated ceiling at worst (the ceiling is rock), so a click always finds something to hang from; a standing piece over a floor hole is refused (nothing below) and `over_hole` covers the data a hand edit makes; a catalog id whose sprite is missing is a test failure, not a runtime check; changing a room's area never rewrites decor.
- **Start.** The start never leaves the floor: `start_floor` stays exact; moving the start into a room whose floor is entirely gated or holed is refused for want of a valid stand; the old start room keeps its content; the validator's "exactly one start" holds after every step, including after Undo (one snapshot spans all rooms it changed).
- **Shrink.** Left and top shrink shift content back by whole screens, so world positions never move (the Grow test, inverted); an exit on a perpendicular edge shifts and its partner does not; a shrink after a grow of the same side restores the room exactly when nothing was added (a round-trip test).
- **Delete.** Deleting a room that a shortcut switch in another room opens leaves that switch orphaned (lint says so); deleting a room that is the only route to others leaves them unreachable (the validator says so); Undo restores the room and every partner exit; deleting the room you are standing in opens the room you came from, or the first by id.
- **Save.** A deleted source room's file is removed only inside `save_dir`; a removal failure is reported like a write failure and leaves the room recorded for the next Save; saving never removes a file the model did not list.
- **Play kit.** An empty kit plays as P2's plain Play did; a kit the validator rejects is impossible to build from the controls; the kit survives the Play round trip with the other options; `Cave movement` is idempotent.
- **Panels and focus.** Number controls are `SpinBox`es whose inner `LineEdit` commits on focus loss, so the P2 `_commit_pending` rule covers them; dropdowns and checkboxes are `FOCUS_NONE`; Tab, Delete, Backspace and the clipboard keys are ignored while typing (Cmd/Ctrl+S still saves).
- **JSON export.** Output is stable-ordered (rooms by id, findings in rule order) so CI diffs are readable; an empty list prints `[]` and exits 0.

## Rulings I made

1. **Dressing is not edited in P3.** The shipped game builds rooms with `RoomBuilder.simple_layers := true`, which skips the parallax stack and `SetDressing` entirely: the roughly 110 dressing entries in the data are not drawn in play today. A tool to place scenery nobody can see would be editing data with no effect, and the editor's own view would have to build a second, fake parallax to show it. Dressing waits for the day `simple_layers` goes off (P4). Cost if wrong: Sean edits dressing by hand until then. Decor, which *is* drawn, is in.
2. **The Start tool moves the default pool with the start.** The validator requires the pool whose id is `WorldProgress.DEFAULT_POOL` in the start room, and that id (`C1`) is a name the editor's generated ids never produce, so the pool cannot be recreated by pasting. Moving it is the only way to move the world's start without leaving the world invalid. Cost if wrong: a refused move when no floor spot fits; the author places the start by hand.
3. **Deleting a room is allowed and removes its file on Save.** P1 forbade it ("git is the safety net"); the same safety net covers a deleted file, and a room made by mistake is otherwise permanent until a shell. The start room cannot be deleted. Cost if wrong: a shipped room deleted by accident is restored from git; the content pins on `ShippedRooms.IDS` go red and tell him.
4. **Shrink refuses rather than deletes.** A shrink that silently removed the content in the strip is a data-loss path that Undo is the only guard for. Cost if wrong: one extra move-or-delete before a shrink.
5. **No biome-match lint for decor.** Cave decor in a `deep` room is a design choice (a shared prop), not an error, and the catalog's biome prefix is a convention, not a rule. Only an id with no sprite is a finding. Cost if wrong: an unintended mixed-biome room passes Validate.
6. **Typed numbers are not snapped.** The grid is for drags; numeric fields exist for the cases the grid cannot express (a ledge at y 266 in C1). Cost if wrong: an off-grid value the next drag snaps.
7. **One element on the clipboard, no multi-select.** A group move is P4's prefab stamp. Cost if wrong: repeated copy and paste for a cluster.
8. **`gate` stays a label.** Enforcing it in play (a closed door that opens with Wall Cling) is gameplay, and P4's reachability is what reads it. Cost if wrong: a gated exit that is not actually gated until then; it already is not today.
9. **Cut leaves the element in the clipboard.** A cut then a paste in rock refuses and the element is still safe to paste elsewhere; a cut that emptied the clipboard on a refused paste would lose it. Cost if wrong: a stale clipboard after the author gives up.
10. **The ruler models the base jump only.** The lint's own `REACH_*` constants are the single source of the numbers, so the overlay and the rule cannot disagree; abilities are P4's surface model. Cost if wrong: a Wall Cling room looks unreachable under the ruler.
