# The opening as a JRPG battle: build record and final review (2026-10-04)

Branch `feat/opening-battle`, from Sean's note that the opening "does not look anything like the rest of the game in terms of art style", then "it is ok for the opening to be brighter or more hopeful" and "look at a few isekai openings". Bounded change to the existing opening (plan 4): the presentation, the art and the copy changed; `OpeningModel`, the validator and the hand-off to her menu did not.

## What was built

- A side-view JRPG battle on a sunny, blossom-lined street (genre check: isekai openings use a bright ordinary street, a white flash at impact, then a white room; the rainy night street first proposed was dropped). The commuter stands right, the truck rolls in from the left, a message window runs along the top and the command and status windows (the voice's pop-up style) along the bottom.
- Five commands: Fight, Dodge, Jump, Pray, Run. Fight (a feeble umbrella swing) is new; `OpeningDef.MAX_CHOICES` is 5. The shipped copy has 15 placeholder result lines.
- Each of the three trucks is one round: INTRO, COMMAND, ACTION, RESULT (read it), HIT (HP 30 drops by a third), then KO and a fade to white that carries over her menu. Enter finishes any animation; nothing skips the scene.
- Art generated with the repo's Codex pipeline on 2026-10-04, one image per frame on a magenta key: 16 commuter frames (idle 2, fight 3, dodge 2, jump 2, pray 2, run 3, hurt, ko), 4 truck frames (idle 2, charge 2) and the street backdrop, assembled into `assets/sheets/commuter.*` and `truck.*` and `assets/opening/backdrop.png`, played through the creatures' `SpriteSheet` and `SlimeAnimator` via the new `OpeningActor` and `data/opening_clips.json`. Tools: `tools/art/commuter_frames.json`, `truck_frames.json`, `opening_prompts.json`, `opening_gen.py`, `opening_build.py`.
- Scale: the generator fits every pose to its canvas, so each frame's width carries a per-pose correction found by comparing head sizes (about 0.9 for most poses, 0.8 for kneeling), with the standing commuter 86 px tall and the truck 247 px wide (both 1.3 times a first pass that looked too small).

## Rulings

- The bestiary page lists every sheet in `assets/sheets`, so the two battle sprites tripped its test. `tools/build_bestiary.py` gained `NOT_CREATURES = {"commuter", "truck"}` and its test reads that list: a small edit to the movement agent's tool. Cost if wrong: the commuter and truck would appear on the page, or the list is removed.
- HP is a constant 30 and each truck takes `ceil(30 / trucks)`, so the third always knocks him out. Cost if wrong: tune `MAX_HP`.
- The white fade carries over her menu (`finished` is emitted with the white full, then it fades away), instead of cutting from white to her dark menu.
- Declined (pentester, "repository art prompts become instructions to a Codex process"): the generator is meant to feed committed prompts to Codex, exactly like `terrain_gen.py` and `generate_frames.py`, with `-a never` and the workspace-write sandbox; anyone who can change the JSON can change the scripts too. The piece-name traversal in the same finding was real and fixed.
- Declined (cartographer): the design doc's dated "Decided (2026-10-04): a short, rigged, turn-based dodge" line is a decision record and stays accurate; the "Built" paragraph below it describes the battle.
