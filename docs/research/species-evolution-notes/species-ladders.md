# Evolution ladders and archetype variants: pattern library (slime, spider, goblin, undead, wolf)

Method notes
- Design doc read first (Creature design, Essence/levels/skills/evolution, Powers). Proposals below respect its decisions: slime lineages weaver/tide/toxic/bulwark/echo/greater with a converging stage 4; undead tree skeleton -> zombie -> ghoul -> vampire, plus archer and lich paths steered by grafts; goblin learns instead of eating, tamer/paladin/assassin wanted; spider poison = water + dark; fire, mind and blood arrive later.
- Tags: [read] = page fetched and read this run (fetches were summarised by a small model; nothing here is an exact quote, and I paraphrase). [inf] = my inference or memory, not backed by a fetched page. [gap] = tried, nothing usable. There are NO [snippet] tags: the WebSearch budget was exhausted (200/200) before I could search, so every web claim comes from a fetched page.
- Suggestion lines ("for us") are always [inf]. Tags mid-line apply to the clause before them.

## 1. SLIME

1. Dragon Quest slime family (Wikipedia): standard blue teardrop slime, she-slime (orange, a bit stronger), bubble slime (pool-like), nautical slime (conch shell), healslime (tentacles), emperor slime (gem shape), box slime (cube), king slime (crown). [read] For us: one silhouette, many props and palette swaps; the prop IS the role. [inf]
2. Metal slime (Wikipedia, dragon-quest.org): silver body, very low HP, very high defence, high evasion, flees on sight, pays out a very large XP reward; liquid metal slime derives from the bubble-slime look; metal king slime is the crowned giant. [read] For us: a rare, fleeing, high-payout slime-type enemy is a cheap essence jackpot and teaches chasing with the bouncer's mobility. [inf]
3. King slime merge (dragon-quest.org): a king slime forms when eight slimes gather; a slime stack forms when three slimes join (added in DQ IX). [read] For us: the "greater" stage 4 can read as many small slimes fused, with visible smaller slimes inside or riding on top. [inf]
4. Vicious slime (dragon-quest.org, DQ XI): ordinary slimes driven to dangerous extremes by dark power; uses spells; much higher stats. [read] For us: the same body corrupted by dark is a clean "elite variant" for demon area and crypt. [inf]
5. Terraria slimes by biome (terraria.wiki.gg): green/blue/red/purple/yellow by depth; jungle slime (spiked version fires jungle spikes), ice slime (chills), sand slime, lava slime (floats in lava, fire immune), black slime (darkness), corrupt slime (spawns slimelings on death), crimslime, illuminant slime (glows), rainbow slime, toxic sludge (only slime with a visible face). [read] For us: biome = element reading; a spiked sub-form per element matches bulwark/earth. [inf]
6. King slime and queen slime (terraria.wiki.gg): crown, splits off blue slimes as it takes damage, shrinks to about 60 percent as HP falls, a ninja is visible inside its body, drops a slimy saddle; queen slime is the hardmode Hallow version. [read] For us: swallowed things sit visibly inside the jelly, which already matches the doc's held-part slots. [inf]
7. Gelatinous cube (Wikipedia): ten-foot transparent cube, dungeon scavenger, digests organic matter with acid, expels what it cannot digest, items and remains visible inside its body, squeezes through corridors then resumes shape. [read] For us: the strongest precedent for "parts sit inside the body, indigestible ones stay" and for a hoarder slime. [inf]
8. Pathfinder ooze class (pathfinderwiki): amorphous, mostly protoplasm, many acid attackers immune to acid, arise from failed alchemy or natural evolution. [read] Page lists no named oozes; black pudding / ochre jelly splitting-on-damage is my memory only. [inf]
9. Goomy -> Sliggoo -> Goodra (pokemondb): dragon slime line, Goodra needs rain and level; Hisuian Sliggoo -> Hisuian Goodra branch is Steel/Dragon with a hard shell, more defence, less speed; Goodra hugs leave the owner covered in slime. [read] For us: same body, regional branch = harder shell and slower; condition-gated evolution (rain) fits the tide lineage. [inf]
10. Grimer -> Muk and Alolan Muk (pokemondb): Muk is pure Poison; Alolan Muk is Poison/Dark, smaller but heavier, more colourful from eating trash, quieter and friendlier. [read] For us: the diet changes the element; eating junk reading dark is a ready model for toxic slime. [inf]
11. That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime (Wikipedia): the Predator skill devours anything and mimics its appearance and skills; he gains a human form after devouring a deceased human. [read] Evolution stage names (demon slime etc.) were not returned by the page. [gap] For us: "eat to copy a form or a part" is already the doc's slime verb; do not copy the series' ladder. [inf]
12. Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow (Wikipedia): nearly every enemy can yield a soul, absorbed into red bullet, blue guardian (continuous effects, familiars, transformations), yellow enchant (passives) and ability souls from unique enemies. [read] For us: part slots could be typed active / continuous / passive. [inf]
13. Monster Hunter, Dungeon Crawl and Digimon slime-like lines not retrievable (see Gaps). [gap]

## 2. SPIDER

1. So I'm a Spider, So What? (Wikipedia): the protagonist goes from a nameless spider through a small poison taratect form, a zoa ele form (forelegs sharp as knives, better combat and stealth), an arachne form (half-human, can speak with humans) and meets queen taratects; evolution is triggered by level and shaped by skill choices; web, poison, detection, appraisal and wisdom are the core skills. [read] For us: stage names that change body plan (sharp limbs, then humanoid upper body) are the pattern; skills decide the branch. [inf]
2. Jorogumo (Wikipedia): a shapeshifting spider woman who lures men in a beautiful human form, entangles them with silk near waterfalls, and devours them; venom is potent. [read] The page gave no age-based power-up rule. [gap] For us: a disguise form is a gate tool (pass as human or goblin). [inf]
3. Arachne (Wikipedia): the weaver who challenged Minerva (Athena) and was turned into a spider; the myth explains why spiders spin webs. [read] For us: weaving as craft identity supports a "weaver" branch. [inf]
4. Drider and Lolth (Wikipedia): drow upper body on a giant spider lower body, made as punishment or as a dark ascension by the Spider Queen Lolth (a black widow with a drow head); keeps drow magic and intellect, adds climbing and webs; giant spiders are drow war animals; phase spiders and ettercaps live in the same territory. [read] For us: the capstone can be a humanoid-hybrid with better item use. [inf]
5. Terraria Wall Creeper (terraria.wiki.gg): two AIs, spider AI while climbing walls and fighter AI on flat ground; cannot pass 2-tile-wide shafts like all spiders; resistant to poison. [read] For us: the crawler enemy already needs a ground fallback; level geometry can wall spiders out. [inf]
6. Terraria Black Recluse (terraria.wiki.gg): Hardmode spider nest enemy, 10 percent chance of an acid venom damage-over-time debuff, spits a web that immobilises (Expert and above), replaces wall creepers in nests. [read] The page does not say Jungle Creeper is a later tier of the same line; it is a jungle variant. [read] For us: web-spit immobilise plus venom tick is the doc's own kit, so a tier up means stronger poison, not a new verb. [inf]
7. Terraria spider roster (terraria.wiki.gg): blood crawler, jungle creeper, wall creeper, black recluse, giant spider, queen spider; the page is a disambiguation list with no descriptions. [read] For us: variant by biome rather than by upgrade is the cheap way to fill areas. [inf]
8. Spinarak -> Ariados (pokemondb): Bug/Poison, evolves at level 22, spins string from both mouth and rear, abilities Insomnia / Sniper / Swarm, moves String Shot and Poison Jab. [read] For us: a first evolution that mainly adds reach (more thread sources) is a gentle stage 1 to 2 step. [inf]
9. Joltik -> Galvantula (pokemondb): Bug/Electric; Electroweb from level 1; Sticky Web on evolution; electrified threads spat as a barrier; sets traps of electric thread near bird nests. [read] For us: web + lightning (light + air) is an obvious second web element and a trapper identity. [inf]
10. Pathfinder spiders (pathfinderwiki): giant spider (CR 1), spider swarm, goliath spider "bigger than a house". [read] Phase spider blinking is my memory only. [inf] For us: "swarm" as a form (many small spiders) is a summoner or broodmother hook. [inf]
11. Drider page also lists the Spider Queen as ruler of the Demonweb Pits. [read] For us: a stage 4 queen/mother form that commands spiders fits the demon area. [inf]
12. Taboo/immortality/dual-perception skills (Wikipedia, spider novel): later skills add multitasking and rebirth by egg-laying. [read] For us: cocoon-laid eggs as a respawn or ally mechanic. [inf]

## 3. GOBLIN

1. Terraria goblin army (terraria.wiki.gg): peon (breaks doors rather than opens them), thief, warrior, archer, sorcerer, and the Hardmode-only warlock; a goblin tinkerer becomes a town NPC after the army is beaten (reforge service); goblin scout drops tattered cloth. [read] Role-by-name (melee, fast, tank, ranged, caster) is my reading of the names; the page's per-unit traits were partly echoed back by the summariser. [inf]
2. Terraria goblin warlock (formerly goblin summoner): summons a shadowflame apparition, flies to reposition, fires chaos balls, very high HP and knockback resistance; ranks above the sorcerer. [read] For us: summoner is the top of a caster branch; dark element on a goblin reads as shadowflame. [inf]
3. Goblin tinkerer (terraria.wiki.gg): the enemy race gives you a friendly crafter after you defeat its army. [read] For us: a goblin form can be the player-side version of this crafter. [inf]
4. Pathfinder goblins (pathfinderwiki): fire fascination and pyromania, songs, fear of dogs and horses, ride wolves and wargs, crude improvised blades (dogslicers) and polearms (horsechoppers), goblin dogs as mounts; variants rasp, forest, monkey (arboreal), snow, and dokkaebi (shapeshifters). [read] For us: fire is a natural goblin element, late; biome variants are a free reskin set. [inf]
5. Pathfinder hobgoblins (pathfinderwiki): regimented military, strong and cunning, keen engineers and alchemists (burning oil, rigged mineshafts, explosives), advancement by duels. [read] For us: the disciplined tinker/engineer upgrade of a scrappy goblin; supports a bomber branch. [inf]
6. Pathfinder bugbears (pathfinderwiki): about seven feet, stealthy for their size, hunt by fear; variants kardans (hide in plain sight), wikkawaks (arctic, no tracks), murds (swamp), slate-stalkers (urban), koblaks (undead-touched apex), frightful haunters (incorporeal fear-hunters); a myth says the first bugbear was born to goblins and banished. [read] For us: swamp, snow and urban sub-variants show how one archetype is re-skinned per area; assassin = fear and ambush. [inf]
7. Warcraft goblins (warcraft.wiki.gg): cartel traders, rocket boots and packs, bombs, shredders, zeppelins; warlocks use fel as cheap renewable power, priests channel the Light for fees, shamans bargain with elementals; hobgoblins are mutated, bigger and dumber; gilgoblins are aquatic. [read] For us: light on a goblin can start as greed or a bargain and be redeemed, matching the doc's paladin idea. [inf]
8. Goblin-kin in folklore (Wikipedia Goblin): hobgoblin (household spirit), bugbear, redcap (iron-shod border murderer), boggart (shapeshifting prankster), kobold; D&D lists goblin, hobgoblin, bugbear and worg riders; Warcraft/Warhammer goblins are engineers. [read] For us: a goblin line can borrow redcap (blood later) as an assassin-ish capstone. [inf]
9. Kobold (Wikipedia): house kobold (chores for offerings, cruel if slighted), mine kobold (cobalt metal is named after it), ship kobold (klabautermann). [read] For us: a transactional creature that works for payment matches taming with offered items. [inf]
10. Goblins on wolves (Wikipedia Goblin, Warg): worg riders are mounted goblin cavalry; worgs are intelligent mounts for goblinoids. [read] For us: wolf companion is the obvious tamer pet and links goblin and wolf trees. [inf]
11. Pathfinder bugbear ghast link (pathfinderwiki Ghoul): ghasts rise when stronger creatures such as bugbears become ghouls. [read] For us: a goblin that dies badly could feed the undead graft pool. [inf]
12. Tensura goblin to hobgoblin evolutions: the Wikipedia fetch did not carry them. [gap]
13. Dungeon Crawl, D&D SRD and Warhammer goblin entries: blocked or not fetched. [gap]

## 4. UNDEAD

1. Pathfinder skeleton varieties (pathfinderwiki): skeletal champions keep some intelligence, bloody skeletons heal from gore, burning skeletons explode as fireballs, acid / electric / frost skeletons, compact skeletons from small races, monstrous skeletons from beasts, multi-armed whirlwind skeletons, multiplying skeletons that split smaller, sturdy skeletons from dwarves and orcs. [read] For us: the source body changes the skeleton, which is the graft mechanic as variants. [inf]
2. Terraria skeleton roster (terraria.wiki.gg): many named skeletons (headache, misassembled, pantless, seasonal costume variants), more than 50 kinds in all. [read] For us: cosmetic body-part variants cost little and show growth. [inf]
3. Terraria necromancer (terraria.wiki.gg): Hardmode dungeon caster, fires shadow beams that bounce off walls, slows the target, teleports every few seconds, armored version has more HP. [read] For us: a lich-path caster that fights with bouncing beams and blinks. [inf]
4. Pathfinder ghoul family (pathfinderwiki): ghoul created by bite fever or cannibalism; ghast has paralysing touch and sickening stench and rises from stronger victims; lacedon is aquatic; leng ghoul has a canine snout and hooves. [read] For us: ghoul stage 3 gets paralysis touch and stench; a swamp lacedon variant. [inf]
5. Arabic ghul (Wikipedia Ghoul): shapeshifts into the person most recently eaten, lures travellers, lives in graveyards, avoids daylight; Warcraft ghouls are hunched clawed servants; Fallout ghouls are decaying mutated humans. [read] For us: copying the last eaten form is a graft-flavoured verb. [inf]
6. Pathfinder wight and wraith (pathfinderwiki): wights arise from violent death or evil souls, variants brute, cairn, frost; wraiths are light-hating, envious, and become dread wraiths after consuming enough life force. [read] For us: drain to rank up is exactly the undead essence method. [inf]
7. Pathfinder vampires (pathfinderwiki): moroi, jiang-shi, nosferatu, vetala; vampire spawn are lesser than full-blood vampires; dhampir are half-blood. [read] Folklore list (Wikipedia Vampire): strigoi, vrykolakas, upir, jiangshi (hopping corpses that absorb life essence), aswang, penanggalan (detachable head), asanbosam (iron teeth, tree-dwelling). [read] For us: spawn -> full vampire = a stage ladder; a detachable-head or hopping variant is a cheap silhouette gag. [inf]
8. Lich and demilich (Wikipedia, pathfinderwiki): a lich keeps its soul in a phylactery or soul cage and returns until it is destroyed; a demilich is what remains after the cage is destroyed, a withered mind in an animate skull with intact power; Warcraft liches top the undead ranks. [read] For us: phylactery maps to altar and remains, a skull capstone is frail but strong. [inf]
9. Warcraft undead roster (warcraft.wiki.gg): ghouls (foot soldiers), abominations (stitched from corpse parts, heavy melee), banshees (wailing control), death knights (armoured elite), liches (casters), val'kyr (raise the dead), geists (spectral lesser liches), forsaken (free-willed). [read] For us: abomination is the zombie-graft capstone pattern; banshee is a ranged-control spectral branch. [inf]
10. Draugr (Wikipedia): corporeal grave-dwelling undead, black or blue skin, great strength, shapeshifting and weather control; haugbui stays in its mound and attacks only trespassers; sea draugr is a drowned fisherman with seaweed head. [read] For us: a crypt guardian that never leaves its room is a boss-room pattern; sea draugr for the swamp. [inf]
11. Revenant (Wikipedia): a corporeal returned corpse driven to harass or avenge, relentless until destroyed, destroyed by decapitation, burning or holy intervention. [read] For us: a persistent pursuer undead stage fits the "vengeance" fantasy and the holy weakness. [inf]
12. Yamask -> Cofagrigus and Galarian Runerigus (pokemondb): pure Ghost with the Mummy ability that spreads on contact, or Ground/Ghost after a location condition; gold-covered sarcophagus body. [read] For us: a coffin-body form that spreads its trait on contact; area-gated evolution. [inf]
13. Cubone -> Marowak and Alolan Marowak (pokemondb): Marowak is Ground and throws its bone like a boomerang; Alolan Marowak is Fire/Ghost, its bone wreathed in cursed flames (the bones were its mother's). [read] For us: a bone weapon that returns is a skeletal-archer verb; a cursed-fire reading of dark. [inf]
14. Terraria zombies (terraria.wiki.gg): biome variants (frozen, raincoat, spore, maggot in graveyards, swamp the fastest), event variants. [read] For us: zombie variants per area (swamp fast, graveyard maggot). [inf]
15. Zombie lord (pathfinderwiki): a zombie that keeps intelligence and normal speed; Zyphus rot spreads zombification. [read] For us: smarter, faster zombie at the top of a zombie stage. [inf]
16. Holy weakness collected: revenants die to holy intervention, wraiths hate light, ghuls avoid daylight. [read] For us: the cathedral should punish all of these; keep undead unlocks out of it. [inf]
17. Dark Souls hollowing is a player-side degeneration, not an enemy ladder. [read] Dungeon Crawl vampire/ghoul/mummy species pages did not load. [gap]

1. Rockruff -> Lycanroc (pokemondb): one pup splits by time of day into midday (fastest, attacker), midnight (bulkier, reckless, slower), dusk (highest attack, balanced, special ability only on the event pup). [read] For us: a clean fast / tank / hybrid fork keyed to a condition rather than a menu. [inf]
2. Mightyena (pokemondb): travels in packs of about ten, coordinated, defers to a leader, Intimidate lowers foe attack. [read] For us: an Intimidate-style howl debuff and pack calls. [inf]
3. Houndour -> Houndoom (pokemondb): Dark/Fire hellhound, pack leader chosen by fighting (horns swept back), flames mixed with poison, Mega form glows hot. [read] For us: fire + dark is the volcano / demon area wolf. [inf]
4. Fenrir (Wikipedia): grows rapidly, breaks two iron fetters, bound by Gleipnir, destined to break free at Ragnarok and swallow Odin; sons Skoll and Hati chase the sun and moon. [read] For us: a wolf that grows until nothing holds it is a capstone and a chain-breaking gate verb. [inf]
5. Hellhounds (Wikipedia): Cerberus guards the underworld gate and keeps the dead in, Garmr guards Hel's gate, Gwyllgi is a mastiff with red eyes, Cwn Annwn are spectral hunting hounds, some variants burn. [read] For us: gate guardian wolves for cemetery and crypt. [inf]
6. Black dogs (Wikipedia): Black Shuck (malevolent), Barghest (omen of doom), Padfoot, Gytrash (shape-shifting omen), Church Grim (buried at a church to guard sacred ground), Hairy Jack and the Gurt Dog (protectors who guide lost travellers); glowing eyes, silent, huge. 7. Warg and worg (Wikipedia): Tolkien's wargs are intelligent evil wolves ridden by orcs; D&D worgs speak and serve goblinoids; the name carries an outlaw sense. [read] For us: an intelligent, tameable "outcast" wolf for the goblin village link. [inf]
8. Pathfinder wolf kin (pathfinderwiki): wolf, timber wolf, dire wolf (giant primordial), warg, barghest (a fiend resembling wolf and goblin), werewolf; werewolf packs with an alpha, named local packs in one forest. [read] For us: dire = tank rank; barghest shows wolf + goblin hybrids exist. [inf]
9. Werewolf folklore (Wikipedia): Germanic tailless wolf using belts or salves, Slavic vukodlak linked to vampires, Norse ulfhednar wolfskin warriors with pain resistance, Turkic kurtadam voluntary shaman transformation, Haitian lougawou that drains blood, Armenian curse of seven years. 10. Pathfinder lycanthropy (pathfinderwiki): werewolf and wererat most common, packs defend territory, recruit by inflicting lycanthropy. [read] For us: spreading the curse maps to "steal essence without killing". [inf]
11. Terraria werewolf (terraria.wiki.gg): full-moon-only Hardmode enemy that leaps and lunges, can inflict bleeding, takes triple damage from silver bullets, and drops a charm that lets the player transform. [read] For us: a night-only moon form unlocked by a drop. [inf]
12. Leng ghoul (pathfinderwiki): canine snout and hooves on a ghoul; goblin riders use wargs. [read] For us: a wolf-undead hybrid enemy is trivially cheap. [inf]
13. Worgen (Warcraft): the Wikipedia fetch did not carry any lore. [gap]

## 6. CROSS-SPECIES PATTERNS (the three requested categories first)

### 6a. One element read by different species
- Dark: Alolan Muk gains Dark by eating trash (slime); Alolan Marowak reads ghost fire on a bone body (skeleton); the goblin warlock reads it as shadowflame apparitions (goblin); Vicious Slime is slime driven mad by dark power; Houndoom's dark + fire wolf. [read] Spider dark = ambush ceiling stalker (design doc). [inf]
- Fire: Terraria lava slime (immune, floats in lava), Pathfinder burning skeleton (explodes), goblin pyromania, Houndoom flames. [read] Reads as terrain on slimes, death burst on skeletons, thrown bombs on goblins, breath on wolves. [inf]
- Light: Warcraft goblin priests channel the Light for fees (corruptible), Church Grim guards sacred ground (wolf), undead wraiths and ghuls shun light. [read] Reads as a bargain on goblins, a ward on wolves, a wound on undead. [inf]
- Electric / lightning: Galvantula uses electrified thread (spider); Pathfinder electric skeleton. [read] Chaining threads for spiders, a shock aura on bone. [inf]

### 6b. Mid-tree specialisations
- Terraria goblin roster covers melee, thief, tank, archer, caster and summoner as one species. [read] Roles are visible in the sprite and weapon. [inf]
- Lycanroc splits into fast, tanky-reckless and balanced forms off one condition. [read]
- Warcraft undead split by role: foot soldier ghoul, heavy abomination, control banshee, elite death knight, caster lich, reanimator val'kyr. [read]
- Digimon Greymon forks to MetalGreymon or SkullGreymon (Wikipedia Digimon). [read] A fork can also be a risk/reward gamble. [inf]
- Pathfinder bugbear sub-variants by terrain (swamp, arctic, urban) for the same stealth role. [read]

### 6c. Capstones (stage 4 or final)
- Slime: king slime (crown, merge of eight), Terraria king and queen slime, metal king slime. [read]
- Spider: Spider Queen Lolth, queen taratect, drider hybrid. [read]
- Goblin: warlock (summoner, flying), warcraft goblin cartel leaders as engineer-bosses. [read]
- Undead: demilich (skull), nosferatu (ancient, cannot spawn), dread wraith (earned by consuming life), lich as the top Warcraft rank, abomination as a stitched giant. [read]
- Wolf: Fenrir (grows until bound), Mega Houndoom, dire wolf. [read]
- Pattern: capstones usually grow in size or change to a signature silhouette (crown, skull, humanoid hybrid) and gain a leadership or summon verb. [inf]

### 6d. Other reusable patterns
- Regional / environment branch of the same species: Alolan Muk, Alolan Marowak, Hisuian Goodra, Galarian Runerigus, bugbear terrain variants, Terraria slimes and zombies by biome. [read] For us: re-skin per area is the cheap content engine. [inf]
- Evolution gated by a world condition: rain (Goodra), night (Marowak, Midnight Lycanroc), a place (Runerigus), full moon (werewolf). [read] For us: area-linked unlock beats a menu. [inf]
- Body holds visible loot or parts: gelatinous cube, Terraria king slime ninja, abomination from corpse parts, skeletons from other races. [read]
- Drain or eat to rank up: wraith to dread wraith, Predator, Aria soul absorption. [read]
- Leader units: ghast leads ghouls, pack leader Houndoom, alpha werewolf, goblin warchief. [read] For us: companion and tamer tie-in. [inf]
- Holy as a gate for undead: revenants, wraiths and ghuls are weak to holy or light. [read]
- Rare jackpot variant: metal slime. [read]

## 7. [inf] PROPOSALS (drafts; all names are the game's own words)

Common conventions: stages S1-S4; the fork is S3 unless noted; essence mixes use only the five first-pass elements unless marked (later); hosts are areas from the design doc.

### Slime (additions only; the existing six lineages and converging S4 stay)
- Keep: weaver, tide, toxic, bulwark, echo, greater (design doc, read in full). [read] Add two lineages. [inf]
- Stage shape: S1 plain slime, S2 first lineage body, S3 lineage form with its signature verb, S4 the converging greater body that keeps one passive from the lineage taken. [inf]
- Mender (water + light): S2 Dewdrop -> S3 Mender -> S4 converge. Verb: shed healing blobs that heal you and a tamed companion; passive: heal on devouring. Source pattern: healslime. Host: cathedral font. [inf]
- Hoarder (earth + light): S3 Clearbody, translucent so held parts show. Verb: spit an indigestible part out as a projectile; passive: +1 slot and slower digestion. Source pattern: gelatinous cube. Host: goblin village (junk) or cathedral glass. [inf]
- Ember (fire + earth, later): lava-immune, molten trail; host: volcano. [inf]
- S4 "greater" paraphrase: body reads as a stack of small slimes that can split off as decoys. [inf]
- Also add a rare fleeing "mirror slime" enemy as an essence jackpot, found in the cave first and again deeper (metal-slime pattern). [inf]
- Essence reading table: Mender = water + light; Hoarder = earth + light; Ember = fire + earth (later). Light is the new element for slime, so these are the first light lineages. [inf]
- Verb or passive added: Mender "Shed" (heal blobs), Hoarder "Spit Out" (indigestible part as shot) plus slot count, Ember trail. [inf]

### Spider (S1 spiderling, S2 hunter, fork at S3, S4 capstone)
- Shadow-stalker (dark + air): ceiling ambush; verb "Drop" is a silent drop from the ceiling with a bonus strike in dark; passive: unseen on ceilings in dim rooms. Host: crypt. [inf]
- Venomspinner (water + dark = poison): webs apply poison ticks and spread through trap webs, bite adds venom stacking; trapper identity. Host: swamp. [inf]
- Broodmother (earth + dark): cocoons hatch spiderlings that carry prey and harass; summoner, uses the cocoon design; passive: extra cocoon. Host: forest. [inf]
- Optional Sparkweaver (light + air = lightning): web that chains and stuns; host: cathedral. Source: Galvantula. [inf]
- S4: humanoid-hybrid "lure" form that passes as human or goblin in the village (form gate). Source: jorogumo, drider. [inf]
- Stage shape: S1 Spiderling (web zip, trap web), S2 Hunter (stronger poison, longer web), S3 branch form, S4 capstone that keeps the branch verb and adds a second use of cocoons. [inf]
- Essence reading table: Shadow-stalker = dark + air; Venomspinner = water + dark; Broodmother = earth + dark; Sparkweaver = light + air. The design doc already names dark -> shadow-stalker and water + dark -> toxic, so two of these are doc-backed. [inf]
- Spider eats only living things: the Broodmother cocoon holds living prey that hatches as spiderlings, which fits the doc's cocoon-with-prey rule. [inf]

### Goblin (S1 scrapper, S2 scavenger-crafter, fork S3, S4 capstone)
