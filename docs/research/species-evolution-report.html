Make every fork change something. Trees for five species.

What close to 190 pages of evolution and skill-tree design say, checked against your 24 slime forms, then worked examples for the slime, spider, goblin, undead and wolf.

Where the slime stands

Good bones, two soft spots

Six lineages, 24 forms, four stages. The stage-3 choice changes stats and a passive but leads to the same stage 4, and the evolved power does not decide which child you get.

Fix it cheaply: let the power branch pick the stage-3 child, and let stage 4 keep one passive from the child you took. Then the stage-3 choice reaches the ending.

What makes a choice good

Six rules from the precedents

Trade, not upgrade

Slay the Spire

A branch gives something up. Rest or Smith. Hollow Knight charms with limited notches. A fire-aligned spider loses Binding Web because fire burns webs.

Two or three options

Hades, PoE

Never a long menu. Three boons, three cards, three ascendancies per class. Eevee has eight, but each has a clearly different trigger. Five rows is your tree screen's limit.

Joins have two parents

Jogress, duo boons

Exactly two named parents in every convergence you can find: Digimon, Hades, Vampire Survivors, Shin Megami Tensei. Show the missing parent as a ???.

Let place flavour the fork

Eevee, Nocturne

Moss rocks, ice rocks, time of day, the moon. Your areas are the triggers: a cathedral, a swamp, a volcano.

Preview before commit

Nocturne

Show the result first. Nocturne previews a fusion; Vampire Survivors hints pairings; Biomorph lets you try a form where you killed it.

Hide only secrets

Tamagotchi

Hidden conditions push players to wikis. Hide the deepest secrets, show stubs one step ahead. Spore shows the cost of choices with no consequence.

One observation, not a reopening: after a Cave and Grotto clear almost every life is offered the same five lineages. By the sources' definitions that reads as an illusory choice. If you want weight there, three cheap levers exist: offer at most three lineages by diet share, tie an offer to the area where you hit the cap, or let a soul perk lock a form as a head start.

A menu of shapes

Eleven ways to shape a fork

Pick the shape per choice, not per game. Each card names the example in this guide.

Binary branch

exists Two directions off one power. Spider: Web Shot to Snare Bolt or Weave Net.

Three-way fork

norm of three Undead off the skeleton; spider stage 3. Hades, Slay the Spire and Path of Exile all offer three.

Convergence

two parents A Ghoul holding a bone-magic graft becomes a Death Knight. The slime stage 4 already joins two.

Branch decides child

not built Swing Thread leads to Arachne, Binding Web to Snare. The design doc wants it.

Fork by diet

goblin, wolf Mostly beasts learned: Tamer. Mostly junk: Tinker. The dominant lesson picks the form.

Environment-triggered

Eevee, Lycanroc Lava pool and fire essence offers Ember. The area you hit the cap in changes the menu.

Hidden or secret

stub Eat five goblins as a spider: Lure. Eat a mimic three times: Mimic Slime.

Regression

temporary A vampire starved of blood falls back to ghoul until fed. Never remove an earned form.

Temporary fusion

20 s Goblin plus a tamed companion fuse as a Rider. No regret, because it ends.

One-time capstone

once per life Lich or Vampire at the crypt altar for most of your held dark or blood.

Modular body

grafts, parts Ribs, a bow-arm or a skull show on the undead; held parts sit inside the slime.

Four new trees

Spider, goblin, undead, wolf

Solid boxes are the core path, bold outlines are capstones, dashed outlines need an area or element that arrives later, dotted outlines are secrets. Every name is a draft.

Spider: control space, poison, cocoons

section 6
PowersWeb Shot to Snare Bolt or Weave Net. Ground Web to Tripline or Sticky Pit. Venom Fang to Neurotoxin or Necrotic Venom. Cocoon to Brood Sac or Larder.
Trade-offsShadow-stalker is weak in lit rooms. Venomspinner hits softer. Broodmother is slow. Fire burns webs.
HostsCrypt, swamp, forest, cathedral. Lure unlocks by eating goblins and passes as one in the village.

Goblin: improviser, tamer, tinker

section 7
Earned by lessonsBeasts teach the Tamer, junk and fire creatures the Tinker, shadows the Skulker, priests the Paladin.
PowersRock Throw to Sling or Scatter. Junk Trap to Spiked Pit or Net Snare. Offer to Bond or Whistle. Smoke Bomb to Blinding or Choking.
PatternTerraria's goblin army shows one species can hold a full role roster, and its Tinkerer shows an enemy race becoming a friendly crafter.

Undead: three paths and one join

section 8
Grafts steerRibs and flesh toward zombie and ghoul, a bow-arm toward archer, a skull or focus toward lich.
Holy hurtsRevenants, wraiths and ghuls are all weak to holy in the sources. Hold undead unlocks in the crypt, cemetery and swamp; make the cathedral a challenge.
Rank by drainA wraith becomes a dread wraith by consuming life force. A lich that loses its soul cage becomes a skull.

Wolf: pack hunter, guardian, stalker

section 9
Pack bondOne bonded packmate at a time, reusing the goblin's taming. Mightyena's Intimidate becomes a fear howl.
Light readingChurch Grim is a black dog buried to guard sacred ground: the wolf that holds holy ground.
CapstoneFenrir grows until nothing holds it: a verb that breaks bound gates.

Stage 4 keeps one passive

cheap

No new art. Stone from Golem keeps Toughness; Stone from Crystal keeps a reflect. The stage-3 choice is now felt at the end, and the screen can name which parent you came by.

Give each stage-3 form a limit

Dungeon Crawl

Identity comes from restriction. Golem: slower, cannot Leap. Crystal: brittle, cannot regenerate. Brook: cannot hold a heavy part.

Power trees

Small, rich and species-flavoured

Level by effects landing

Mount and Blade

Never on a trigger the player can fire at will. Final Fantasy II players attacked their own party; Skyrim had potion loops. Count effects landing and cap per encounter. Species proficiencies: spider Ceiling Crawl, wolf Pounce, goblin Craft, undead Drain, slime Bounce.

A notch budget

Hollow Knight

Four active slots stay; add passive notches, 3 at stage 1 to 7 at stage 4. Overfed: one extra notch, but a hit knocks out 25% of held essence. Riders and passives swap free at altars; evolutions are fixed for the life.

Capstones change a verb

PoE keystones

Plague Bloom: the Miasma cloud spreads to the next living thing. Anchor Web: one persistent anchor per room to teleport to. Glutton's Debt: hold 20 essence at most, every devour worth double.

Price ladder

Hades Mirror

A branch costs the parent's price, a rider 3 to 4 of a third element, a capstone twice the parent's split over two elements. Later bodies cost 1, 1.5, 2, 3 times, so evolving never becomes free.
