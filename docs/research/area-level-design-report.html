The principles

Six rules that carry into every new area

Each card has the rule, the picture, and the research-doc ID so you can look up the sources.

Loop it, don't append it

A1

Join the new area to the map at two points. Unexplored inserts new sections into an existing cycle; Guacamelee's DLC reused a doorway that was blocked by a cave-in.

Show the lock before the key

A3

Put every gate on or one room off the main route. Super Metroid's glass tunnel and Dead Cells' "hole in the ground you saw in your previous run" both work this way.

Teach alone, then mix

V6

Every timed hazard gets a floor of 21 frames of warning. The 340 ms figure is Dark Souls 3's guaranteed attack signal; other timings are placeholders for you to tune.

Build rooms as rhythm

section 4

Describe a room as a beat, then test it per species. Smith et al. use groups of 5, 10, 15 or 20 s, joined by a small rest platform. A wolf crosses the same room far faster than a slime.

Make danger the loudest thing

V9 FO3

Three tiers: background fades, platforms get an edge, hazards get the brightest colour. Dead Cells and Celeste agree on value contrast plus an outlined walkable edge. They disagree on saturation, so don't rely on it.

Changing species costs a death, so make each rebirth pay. Cluster 2+ gates per species near each other, and give every area lanes for at least two species, rotating which, so none is left out. Each lane gets one visible tile marker, like Dragon's Trap's mouse blocks.

What your engine decides

Four facts that change the design

These came from reading the repo, not the literature. Each one affects both new areas.

The spider walks over every pit

5.1

Every room has a generated 20 px ceiling. A floor hazard is no gate for the spider unless the ceiling is tagged hot or slick, or missing. Decide the slick flag before the volcano.

Open sky removes the spider's lane

5.2

Sky and the spider bypass are one decision. The camera clamps to the room, so sky is art, not space. A canopy band of solids gives the forest a ceiling the spider can use.

A fire death can open a fire gate

5.3
Die in lava→Fire seed starts next life→Fire-keyed gate opens
FixGate on eaten essenceSeed = one-life grace

Your death-seed rule hands over the key. Gate heat_resist on permanent essence and make the seed a short, timed grace, like Terraria's stacked 7 s lava immunities.

Three verbs have nothing to act on

Today's rooms give mantle, vault and the roll's duck almost nothing. New areas need hard ledges, short hard steps and low hard ceilings on purpose: thick branch lips, fallen logs, bramble lines, vent hoods.

Heat is a soft gate, and lava should hurt, not reset you

Super Metroid's Norfair, Metroid Prime and Dread all gate fire areas with heat you can feel before you can beat it.

Part 2: more areas and traps

Seven additions, one rulebook

Cathedral, cemetery, crypt, swamp, goblin village, demon area and traps all need the same missing piece: room data for zones and triggers. Build it once and every area below uses it.

the planning map: nothing is replaced, the Deep becomes a hub (proposed wiring)

Everything is added. The surface gets its own spine, and the Deep gets three exits: the crypt cluster (with the demon area beyond it), the volcano, and the Serpent Lair gate to the last area. The world roughly triples, so build one area at a time and plan region pages for the map tab.

Holy zones: hard, never a lock

SA1-SA4 Z1-Z4

Damage over time with a grace window, not instant death. Every holy-zone precedent has a switch you can reach (NetHack's priest, Nioh's generator, Dark Souls' Undead Mage). V Rising gives "a few seconds" before sun damage and stops it in shade. Each undead form carries a holy_resist, so form changes exposure, not access.

A cemetery that is truly safe

It is a truce, not a consecration. A holy look would tell undead it hurts them. NetHack's sanctuary still lets ranged attackers fire in, so remove or wall off anything with a line of sight. Hollow Knight's Resting Grounds is quiet but holds enemies, so it is not the model.

Swamp: one rule, bog mud

SW1-SW4

Template: Donkey Kong Country 2's Hornet Hole. You cannot walk or attack while stuck, jumping is the only escape, it is taught early, and the spider-like buddy is immune. Maridia is the warning: escaping must never need tuned timing. Mud is a lane modifier, never a gate. Numbers are placeholders.

Goblin village: standing by species

The altar sits outside the palisade. Rebirth is at your chosen altar, so a non-goblin must never respawn inside a hostile village. Standing is derived from your species and a trust flag, never a one-way ratchet (Divinity Original Sin 2's guards "stay hostile no matter what you do"). Any species earns trust in a trial, as in Hollow Knight's Mantis Village.

Lost Izalith is the warning: a theme with no rule and no boss concept. Gauntlet's generators "can be destroyed" gives a rule. The boss door needs only the base-jump gate; verb gates open a shortcut or the rare room. Demon fire stays in the parallax so the volcano keeps fire.

Traps: a trigger plus a hazard

TR1-TR9
trigger: plate, tripwire, sight→telegraph ≥ 340 ms→hazard: dart, blade, drop
invisible trigger + invisible hazard = unfair

plate weight, by species

light: slime, spidermedium: goblin, undeadheavy: wolf only

what a death seeds

nothing: spike, saw, crush, pitfire: jetpoison: gas, dartwater: drowningthe creature's element: mimic, ambush

Traps need a rule for combo deaths: knocked into spikes by a creature should seed the creature's element. Non-lethal traps cost at most 30% of max HP (Dead Cells' cap); lethal ones live in rare rooms. Disarmed traps stay disarmed on every life.

Grow the four existing areas

lever 1

61 of the 233 rooms still to build sit in areas you have already designed (23 now, 84 target). Their themes and creatures exist.

Make rooms bigger

lever 2

Super Metroid's median room is 4 screens, yours is 2. Three-by-one corridors, two-by-two chambers and one-by-three shafts add screens without adding rooms.

Add areas

lever 3

Hollow Knight lists 19 locations; Silksong has 12 areas. Two more areas of about 18 rooms close the gap without growing any other.

Use cheap rooms

lever 4

A quarter of Super Metroid's rooms are a single screen: connectors and item closets. Short rare rooms are the cheapest rooms to build.

What this size changes: one altar per area stops working at 40 to 60 screens per area, so add altars per sub-region or altar-to-altar warping; the map tab needs region pages or a zoom; and tools/world_size.sh prints the live numbers against this table from the room files. It is informational only: it never fails a build.
