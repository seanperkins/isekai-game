# F2: the new areas

What F2 builds and how. Source: `docs/research/area-level-design.md` (the sketches, rules and room budget), `docs/research/species-form-branches.md` section 10 (which creature lives in which area) and `docs/research/items-e2.md` (the buttons and items). F2 ships in pieces, each its own plan, branch and review: **F2a** the builder and the forest, **F2b** swamp and village, **F2c** the sacred cluster (cemetery, cathedral, crypt), **F2d** volcano and demon area, **F2e** the nine deeds that wait on rooms, **F2f** the placement of every creature.

## Built so far

**F2a (built 2026-10-05):** the builder and the forest. `tools/build_areas.sh` writes the rooms (`--check` writes nothing and fails when a room would change); `tools/areas/area_kit.gd` is the kit, `tools/areas/forest.gd` the first module, `tools/areas/areas.gd` the list of modules. Nine forest rooms, FO1 to FO9 (the cells below are the built ones): the Cave Mouth joins C3's right edge (a hop up of 46 from C3's highest ledge, not its top: C3 has no ledge route to its ceiling and a shipped room's solids are not the builder's to change), the Root Hollow drops into C4 and that exit pair carries the label `wall_cling` (one way down; without it C3 and C6 would be reachable without a gate through the forest). Spawns are the four support monsters that name the forest; twins come in F2f. The forest's vertical joins copy the shipped pattern: a zigzag of ledges whose last one stands in the ceiling opening (the Clearing's, as G1's), and a ledge either side of the floor opening above (the Trunk's, as C5's).

**F2b (built 2026-10-05):** the swamp and the goblin village, ten rooms east of the forest. `tools/areas/swamp.gd` (prefix `SW`, five rooms) and `tools/areas/village.gd` (prefix `VI`, four rooms and the Sump). The forest's Den (FO9) opens east onto the Root Seam (SW1, no creatures), the Boardwalk (SW2) crosses to the Bog Flat (SW3), where the Stilt Roost (SW4) climbs above and the Hunting Blind (SW5) lies east; the Bog Flat's floor opens onto the village's Gate Shrine (VI1: a glow pool, no altar, no creatures, so a non-goblin never wakes in a hostile palisade), with the Palisade Roofs (VI2) west of it, the Village Yard (VI3) under both and the Chieftain Hall (VI4) off the Yard. The Yard's floor opens onto the Sump (VI5, area `swamp`, flooded art, a water rect), whose bottom is a second link to the Flooded Tunnels: a hole into F6's top that is a shortcut (`sump_drop`), shut until a swimmer opens it from F6's own highest ledge, where a switch stands (the module attaches it, marked `by`, so the next run strips and rebuilds it). A `swim` label would not hold the hole: a walker sinks through water, drops through F6 and F4 and walks into the Deep. It is a one-way drop; the way back up is the long way round. Spawns are the support monsters that name the area (swamp: blood leech, bog wisp, firefly swarm; village: village goblin, cave rat, junk heap, goblin shaman). The module base gained `link` and `attach_feature`, the kit `AreaKit.climb` (the zigzag up to a ceiling opening) and `AreaKit.flank` (a ledge either side of a floor opening), and `AreaModule.link`. **Art only for now:** mud, murk and the guiding wisps; the village's standing, trust, trial, tame pen and bench. **Not built:** the sketch's verb lanes to the Stilt Roost (zip) and the Hunting Blind (pounce) and the palisade's hard ledges: `GATES` holds only `wall_cling` and `swim`, so every room is reachable on the base jump; the village's one-way drop to the forest Clearing; altars (pools only).

**F2c (built 2026-10-05):** the sacred cluster, fourteen rooms above and east of the Deep: `tools/areas/cemetery.gd` (prefix `CM`), `cathedral.gd` (`CA`, area `sacred`) and `crypt.gd` (`CR`). D7, the antechamber of the Taratect's arena, opens by a door in its west wall onto the Gate Stair (CM4, 1x2 at (17,4): a screen of ledges up) and the Gate Tunnel (CM1, (18,4)) above D7; the door is ungated because the cluster is as far behind the wall cling as D7 is (D2's way into D7), and a hole in D7's ceiling would have no ledge to climb to it (D6, the arena, gains nothing: a boss room has exactly one exit). The Churchyard (CM2, 2x1) is the truce: a glow pool, a tablet, no altar and no spawns; the Lych Gate (CM3) leads east. The cathedral lies north: a climb from the Churchyard up into the Nave (CA1, 3x1, pews), the Transept (CA2) with the Font Chapel (CA3, a pool) beside it, and above the Transept the Bell Shaft (CA4, 1x3), a zigzag to a door onto the Loft (CA5). The crypt lies east: the Crypt Stair (CR1, 1x2) down from the Lych Gate, the Ossuary (CR2), the Niche Gallery (CR3, 3x1) and the Vault (CR4); a hole at the Ossuary's east end drops onto the Coffin Slide (CR5, 2x1), a chute whose own hole drops into D5 (the Deep's dead end gains a top exit): one way down, since D5 has no ledge to climb out by. Spawns are the support monsters that name each area (cemetery: skeleton, ghost, carrion crow; sacred: candle wisp, chapel priest, stained-glass moth, shield guard, gargoyle, ghost; crypt: skeleton, junk heap, vampire bat, bone hound). **Art only for now:** holy beams and the priest's emitter, the safe zone's turn-back, the crypt's curse and dark, the revive link and the traps. **Not built:** the cathedral's species lanes (they need verb gates the validator has no labels for), the cemetery's altar (pools only) and its shortcut, the Churchyard's vista of the nave, a hard-ledge test bed in the Bell Shaft.

**F2d (built 2026-10-05):** the volcano and the demon area, fourteen rooms. `tools/areas/volcano.gd` (prefix `VO`, area `volcano`, seven rooms): a hole in the floor of D4 drops onto Ashfall (a landing with a tablet and no creatures), Ember Hall has the rest pool and the first fire creatures, a stair down the Chimney (a 1x2 shaft) leads to the Forge Row, the hub, with the Magma Chamber (a pool) to the east, the Cooled Vault (a plain side room: `heat_resist` is not a gate label yet, and a label on a floor hole would not hold a walker) below, and the Ash Cut above it, a shaft up to Ember Hall whose ceiling hole is a shortcut (`ash_cut`) shut until the switch inside the Ash Cut is pulled. `tools/areas/demon.gd` (prefix `DM`, area `demon`, seven rooms): the Vault's (CR4) east door leads to the Threshold (a tablet, no creatures) and the Gate Hall (the pool, no creatures, no altar), with three spokes: down to the West (imps and a blinker), up to the Middle (hounds and an imp) and on, level, to the Throne (a 1x2 room, the caller and the imps it calls; a plain room, not a boss arena: a boss room has exactly one exit and a quiet antechamber with a pool), and east through the East (a ward-bearer, an imp and a blinker) to the Reliquary (the area's one rider). Spawns are the support monsters that name each area (volcano: ember beetle, lava salamander, ash moth; demon: imp, demon hound, caller, blinker, ward-bearer, rider). **Art only for now:** heat and lava, eruptions, the quench lane, taint strips and their wards, the three sealed gates (`spawn_gate`), the pact altar and the boss door. **Not built:** the `heat_resist` gate, the species lanes, the second-pass overlay of the Deep, the Throne's shortcut back to the Gate Hall. Every area of the F2 design now has rooms; F2e builds what the deeds need, F2f places the twins.

**F2e (built 2026-10-05):** the nine deeds that waited on rooms. `RoomDef.tags` (`moonlit`, `hold`; lint rule `room_tag`) and five feature kinds, each built by `RoomFeatures.make` into a node that says its deed on the event bus: `RoomBarrier` (a slab: the dissolve verb removes it at once, or three tackles or hits break it, so the wolf form whose gate asks for barriers broken is not locked behind its own verb; either way `barrier_broken` with its id), `RoomHidden` (a wall that looks plain but for a hairline: the reveal verb shows it from a distance, or Inspect beside it searches it, so a form whose gate asks for a hidden room found is not locked behind its own verb; either way `hidden_found` with its id), a barrier broken or a door found this life is not built again that life, `RoomGap` (crossing it airborne, above the ledges either side, says `gap_crossed`), `RoomLamp` (Inspect, "drink": `lamp_drunk`) and `RoomVigil` (ten seconds within 40 px undamaged: `vigil_kept`); `RoomHold` (added to a room tagged `hold`: twenty seconds with a threat alive says `room_held`) and `VillageWatch` (kept by `Game`: leaving the village says `village_kept` when nothing there was killed and the Yard was seen, and `village_visited_unseen` when all four village rooms were seen and nobody there was alerted). Gap, lamp and vigil say themselves once per life per id, and a hold once per life per room, by asking the rules ledger (`SkillRules.count(event, {id})`), so a room built again does not pay twice; their tag `id` is now allowed on those events and on `barrier_broken` and `hidden_found`; the features' size is two plain numbers (`w`, `h`, or `width` for a gap) so that a room spec round trip keeps them, and the room editor and the MCP catalog list the five kinds (`RoomSpec` also reads a room's `tags`). A kill in a `moonlit` room carries `moonlit` (`Player.in_moonlight`, set from the room's tag). **Placed:** moonlit on the forest's six open rooms; barriers across the Hunting Blind's door (SW5) and in the Forge Row's floor gap over the Cooled Vault (VO4); hidden doors at the Font Chapel (CA3) and the Reliquary (DM7); gaps between the Canopy Walk's branches (FO6, two) and the Boardwalk's planks (SW2, two); lamps in the Nave, the Yard and the Ossuary; vigils in the Churchyard, the Font Chapel and the Threshold; `hold` on the Floor Trail, the Nave, the Niche Gallery and the Forge Row. Every one of them stands in front of a dead end or in an optional stretch, never on a way a player must pass. `Deeds.RESERVED` is empty: every deed has an emitter, and no form's gate waits on a piece that is not built. A barrier is a door to a side room, not a shortcut: a room built again puts it back.

**F2f (built 2026-10-05):** the creatures have places. `tools/build_creatures.py` also writes `tools/creature_areas.json`: each of the 169 new creatures' areas and class (80 rare, the table's rare, boss, optional boss, miniboss and guardian; 41 elite; 48 common, the NPCs included, since the game has no neutral creature yet), the 18 lairs (dead ends and optional rooms, one or two to an area) and the 9 quiet rooms (the thresholds, the truce, the rests, D7). `tools/areas/placement.gd` (`AreaPlacement`, prefix `PL`, the last module) places each creature: rares first, then elites, then commons, and within a class the ones with the fewest areas and the biggest first, in the emptiest room of its areas per screen (a rare's lair first while it has room: weighted load under 8 per screen, a common 1, an elite 2, a rare 3), on the surface and at the x where it has the most clear floor around it. A spot has to pass every spawn rule of `RoomLint` (`RoomLint.spawn_findings`) and the creature's drawn size (its sheet's frame, or the creature it wears, times `sprite_size`: the lint cannot see these, no new creature has a sheet of its own): head room (1.1 times its height under a block or the ceiling, 0.7 under a ledge it could jump through), the doorways at the side walls clear, 14 px between neighbours on a level. A flier hovers. The start room, a boss arena and a quiet room get none. The spawns carry `"by": "PL"`, so the builder strips them on every run (a re-run changes nothing), the hand-made spawns and the area modules' own are never touched, and `RoomSpec` round trips the marker so a room edited through the MCP tools is not placed twice. **Result:** 169 creatures in 57 rooms, 342 spawns in the world; the Deep is the fullest (D2, D3 and D4 hold 12 or 13 creatures each in two screens, D5 six in one: 40 creatures live in five rooms), every other room at most five to a screen. **For F3:** the numbers (XP, essence totals, thresholds) are as they were; only the rooms they are met in changed.

## The builder

Rooms stay `RoomDef` resources in `data/rooms/`. The new ones are written by a headless GDScript tool, not by hand: `tools/build_areas.gd` (run by `tools/build_areas.sh`) loads the shipped rooms, builds the rooms each area module in `tools/areas/` describes, attaches them to the shipped world by adding the exits the module names to existing rooms, and saves every changed room with `ResourceSaver` (the same writer the room editor uses, so a re-run changes nothing). An area module builds its rooms with `AreaKit`: `ledge`, `stairs`, `shaft`, `link` (which writes both ends of an exit from one world span), `spawn`, `decor`, `feature`. The tool runs `WorldValidator` and `RoomLint` over the whole world before it writes and refuses to write a world that has a finding.

Art is borrowed (`data/area_art.json`): forest from the grotto, swamp from the flooded tunnels, village, sacred, cemetery and volcano from the cave, crypt and demon from the deep. Decor ids carry the borrowed biome's prefix.

## Where the areas go

The shipped world fills cells x 0 to 23, y 0 to 8. The new areas take free cells (cell = one 640 by 360 screen):

| Area | Cells | Attaches to |
|---|---|---|
| forest | x 3 to 10, y -3 to 1 (built) | C3's right edge (the Cave Mouth), C4's top (a drop) |
| swamp and village | x 11 to 16, y -1 to 4 (built) | the Den's east door, F6's top (the Sump, `swim`) |
| cemetery, cathedral | x 17 to 24, y 0 to 5 (built) | D7's west door (the Gate Stair), behind D7's own wall cling |
| crypt | x 22 to 28, y 4 to 6 (built) | the Lych Gate, and D5 (the Coffin Slide) |
| volcano | x 22 to 26, y 8 to 11 (built) | D4's floor, east end (Ashfall) |
| demon area | x 29 to 35, y 3 to 6 (built) | the crypt's Vault (the Threshold) |

A module may move a room to a free cell if the sketch's shape needs it; `WorldValidator` rejects an overlap.

## Placement of creatures (F2f, built)

Every creature the game has (the 169 new ones) stands in at least one room of one of its areas. `tools/creature_areas.json` (written by `tools/build_creatures.py` from the design table) gives each creature its areas and a class: `common`, `elite`, `rare` (the table's rare, boss, miniboss and guardian) or `npc` (hostile for now: the game has no neutral creature). The placement step gives each room slots by class and puts each creature in the room of its areas with the fewest placed so far (ties by room id), a rare only in a room marked `lair`, each at a floor spot `RoomLint` accepts (`creature_fit`). It adds only spawns whose ids are new creatures and replaces its own on every run, so the hand-authored spawns of the shipped rooms are never touched.

## The nine deeds (F2e, built)

| Deed | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| `defeated {moonlit}` | rooms tagged `moonlit` (the forest's open rooms): the player knows it is in moonlight, as it knows it is in the dark |
| `barrier_broken` | a `barrier` feature: a wall that the dissolve verb removes |
| `hidden_found` | a `hidden` feature (a secret room's door): the reveal verb shows it |
| `gap_crossed` | a `gap` feature: crossing it airborne from one side to the other, once per gap per life |
| `lamp_drunk` | a `lamp` feature: the interact button, once per lamp per life |
| `vigil_kept` | a `vigil` feature: standing at it for 10 seconds undamaged |
| `room_held` | a room tagged `hold`: staying in it 20 seconds with a threat alive, once per room per life |
| `village_visited_unseen` | leaving the village having visited its rooms with no enemy there alerted |
| `village_kept` | leaving the village having killed none of its creatures |

## Not built here

Neutral creatures (the village's merchants and guardians fight like any creature for now), altars with perks in the new areas (pools only), hazard rules beyond what the existing rooms use (mud, heat, taint are art and text until a later piece), and boss arenas.
