Isekai Chronicles — every evolution

All five species, every form from the first body to the last, with the creature each one is in the world, the essences it reads and the monsters the world still needs. A proposal for review: only the forms marked built exist in the game today.

· design: docs/research/species-form-branches.md via tools/build_evolutions.py · design review

How to read this
  • Form 1 is a species' first body. It splits into three or four form 2 lines, each into two form 3s and each of those into two form 4s. Branches never rejoin.
  • ★ marks the four asks: the werewolf, the secret light undead, the heavy-weapon undead and the half-spider. Built forms exist in the game. Secret forms need a hidden condition. A dashed chip is an essence no creature carries yet (fire, mind, blood): that form shows as a stub until it arrives.
  • World twin: every form is also a creature the player can meet, with the same kit. Its tier sets the essence it carries and the XP it pays. Click any form for its twin.
  • The numbers are the design's, not tuned: the essence amounts follow the shipped creatures' scale, and the thresholds that open slime lineages are calibrated in the document.
